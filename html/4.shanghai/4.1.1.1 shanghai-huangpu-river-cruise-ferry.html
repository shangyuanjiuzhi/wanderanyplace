Huangpu River Cruise & Ferry

Complete guide to experiencing the Bund skyline from the water

Huangpu River Ferry Guide

Ferry Guide

1. Search Location

Search "Jinling Donglu Pier" or "金陵东路渡口" on your map.

2. Ferry vs Cruise

When you arrive, remember: the 2 RMB ferry is on the right. The left side is the pricier Huangpu River Cruise, so don't mix them up.

3. Payment Methods

You can pay in many ways - cash (no change given), your metro transportation card, or Alipay/WeChat linked to your bank card.

4. Operating Hours

Operating hours are usually from around 7 AM to 6:30 PM. Service may pause during bad weather, so keep an eye on conditions. Enjoy your 2 RMB mini-cruise!

Huangpu River Cruise

Online Purchase (WeChat Mini Program)

  • Search "上海黄浦江游览" in WeChat
  • Select your preferred cruise line and time
  • Book and pay directly through the mini program
  • Show QR code at the pier for boarding
  • Price range: ¥100 - ¥200 per person

Offline Purchase (Shipu Pier)

  • Go to Shipu Tourist Wharf (十六铺旅游码头)
  • Located at No. 500 Zhongshan East Road
  • Buy tickets directly at the ticket counter
  • Cash, Alipay, and WeChat Pay accepted
  • Recommended to arrive 30 minutes before departure

Remark: Source from Xiaohongshu (Little Red Book)
