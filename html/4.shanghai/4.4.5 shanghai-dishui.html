Dishui Lake Overview

A comprehensive guide to Shanghai's beautiful artificial lake in Lingang New City.

Dishui Lake is Shanghai's largest artificial lake, located in the Lingang New Area of Pudong. With a diameter of 2.5 kilometers and covering an area of about 5.5 square kilometers, this stunning lake features a unique circular shape with three small islands at its center. Surrounded by lush greenery and modern architecture, it offers a peaceful escape from the bustling city.

The lake is a popular destination for outdoor activities, including cycling, jogging, picnicking, and boating. The surrounding area includes several parks, shopping centers, and cultural facilities, making it a great place for a day trip or weekend getaway.

Dishui Lake is especially beautiful at sunset, when the golden light reflects on the water, creating a magical atmosphere. The lake is also home to various events throughout the year, including music festivals, sports competitions, and cultural exhibitions.

Recommended Tour Route

Recommended Route: Duoyun Bookstore → Dishui Lake Celebration Square → Huangri Harbor → No. 1 Pier → Yifu·li → Dishui Lake Camping

  1. Duoyun Bookstore - Features 270° floor-to-ceiling windows offering panoramic views of Dishui Lake. The other side is lined with towering bookshelves filled with books, creating a spectacular atmosphere.
  2. Dishui Lake Celebration Square - Hosts concerts on weekends and holidays. Many people gather around 7 PM to enjoy the shows.
  3. Huangri Harbor - A scenic harbor area with beautiful waterfront views, perfect for a leisurely walk.
  4. No. 1 Pier - A modern pier offering great photo opportunities with the lake and surrounding architecture.
  5. Yifu·li - A popular photo spot with a cool Korean-style aesthetic. Many visitors love taking photos here.
  6. Dishui Lake Camping - Ideal for spring and summer camping. A large unobstructed campsite perfect for barbecues, tents, pets, and families. Catch a beautiful sunset if you're lucky.

Bonus Tip: Don't miss the weekly fireworks show! Every Saturday at 20:00-20:06 (6 minutes). Best viewing spots: Cherry Blossom Island, subway station area, or the east bank near Yichi Garden.

Detailed Information

Location

Dishui Lake, Lingang New Area, Pudong District, Shanghai

Opening Hours
  • Lake Area: Open 24 hours
  • Park: 6:00 - 21:00
  • Bicycle Rental: 8:00 - 18:00
Best Time to Visit

October - November (spring-like weather) and March - May

Evening visits (19:00-21:00) for light shows

Tickets

Free: Entrance to the lake area

Bicycle Rental: ¥30-50 per hour

Boat Rental: Varying fees

Reservation Information
  • No reservation needed for general visit
  • Bicycle rental available on site
Transportation
  • Metro: Line 16 to Dishui Lake Station
  • Bus: Buses 1009, 1043 to Dishui Lake
  • Car: 40-minute drive from Pudong Airport
