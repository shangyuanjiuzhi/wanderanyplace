About Shanghai Walking

Shanghai's streets are a fascinating blend of East and West, tradition and modernity. From the historic Bund with its colonial architecture to the futuristic skyline of Lujiazui, every corner tells a unique story.

A walk through Shanghai's neighborhoods is like exploring a living museum. You'll encounter hidden alleyways, trendy cafes, art galleries, and local street food stalls that capture the city's dynamic spirit.

Our curated walking routes focus on Shanghai's most iconic and charming neighborhoods. Each route offers a unique perspective on the city's culture, history, and contemporary life.

Line 2 3-4 hours | 1.5-2 km

愚园路 Yuyuan Road

Yuyuan Road is a hidden gem that combines historical charm, trendy shops, and artistic vibe. It's perfect for a leisurely half-day stroll. Here's a non-backtracking Citywalk route and guide. Recommended to walk from east to west, covering about 1.5-2 km, and taking approximately 3-4 hours to explore and enjoy local food.

Route Map

Line 2: 愚园路 Yuyuan Road - From Jiangsu Road to Zhongshan Park

1
Jiangsu Road Subway Station (江苏路地铁站)

Starting point: Exit 4, Line 2/11

🛍️ Recommended Shops:
1、Artist La Pie: A French bakery in an old villa, perfect for afternoon tea
2、Money Shops: A Western restaurant in a standalone villa with sunny outdoor seating
2
Changning Children's Palace (长宁区少年宫)

Former Wang Mansion, a stunning Gothic castle-style building with red bricks and pointed roofs

🛍️ Recommended Shops:
1、Yuyuan Public Market: Old factory turned market with authentic Shanghai snacks on 1F
3
Yongquan Fang (涌泉坊)

Classic Shanghai new-style lilong with Spanish architecture, known as one of "Shanghai's Most Beautiful Lilongs"

4
Qishan Village (岐山村)

Former residence of Qian Xuesen, Shi Zhecun and other celebrities, with well-preserved old Shanghai lilong style

5
Zhongshan Park Subway Station (中山公园地铁站)

End point: Exit 8, Line 2/3/4, or continue walking to Jing'an Temple

Line 4 2-3 hours | 3-4 km

武康路 & 安福路 Wukang Road & Anfu Road

Experience Shanghai's French Concession romance. Wukang Road & Anfu Road Citywalk combines old Shanghai's vintage charm with modern trendiness. This 1.5km route takes 3-4 hours, capturing the essence of Shanghai.

Route Map

Line 4: Wukang Road -> Anfu Road

1
Wukang Building (武康大楼)

Start from Exit 7, Jiaotong University Station (Line 10/11). 5-min walk to this iconic landmark shaped like a ship at 5-road intersection. Best photo spots: Old Wheat Cafe crosswalk & Wukang Building Post Office.See details here

2
Wukang Road (武康路)

A living architectural museum with French sycamore trees and historic villas. Key spots: Ba Jin Former Residence (113 Wukang Rd), Romeo's Balcony (210 Wukang Rd), Wukang Yard - a restored courtyard with galleries & cafes

3
Anfu Road (安福路)

Shanghai's trend hub with fashionistas, street photographers, and hip boutiques.

🛍️ Recommended Shops:
1、Duozhuoyu Secondhand Store: 300 Anfu Rd - Trendy secondhand fashion
2、Sunflour Bakery: 308 Anfu Rd - Popular bakery with delicious pastries
3、RAC Bar: 322 Anfu Rd - Stylish bar & restaurant
4、HARMAY: Industrial-style beauty store
4
Route Extensions

Option A: Sinan Road (15-min walk) - quiet vintage vibe with garden villas & Sinan Bookstore. see detail here

Option B: Huaihai Middle Road - seamless transition from artsy to commercial, visit TX Huaihai for latest trends

Tips for Shanghai Walking

Best Time

Early morning or late afternoon to avoid crowds and enjoy the best lighting for photos.

Getting Around

Most neighborhoods are accessible by subway. Consider using shared bikes to cover longer distances.

Etiquette

Respect local residents' privacy. Ask permission before taking photos of people or private spaces.
