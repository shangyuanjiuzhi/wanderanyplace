Jing An Temple Overview

One of Shanghai's most famous Buddhist temples, located in the heart of Jing'an District. Features a stunning golden pagoda and beautiful traditional architecture, contrasting with the surrounding modern skyscrapers.

Jing An Temple is one of Shanghai's most famous Buddhist temples, located in the heart of Jing'an District. This historic temple stands in stark contrast to the modern skyscrapers that surround it, creating a unique blend of ancient and contemporary.

What makes Jing An Temple special is its golden pagoda, which shines brightly against the Shanghai skyline. The temple features beautiful traditional architecture and is a peaceful oasis in the bustling city center. It's also famous for its annual temple fair during Chinese New Year.

Blessing Route

  1. Incense - Scan QR code on the right side after entering to purchase incense for ¥10.
  2. Shanmen Hall - Front: Maitreya Bodhisattva (for peace); Back: Wei Tuo Bodhisattva (for protection from disasters).
  3. Mahavira Hall - Main hall with a 15-ton pure silver Sakyamuni Buddha. Pray for family peace and disaster relief.
  4. Guanyin Hall - Pray for good relationships, family peace, and health.
  5. Muni Hall - Pray for health and spiritual purification.
  6. Coin Toss - Try tossing coins at the stone pillar in front of Mahavira Hall. Hitting the lucky spot means good fortune.
  7. Dharma Items Shop - Left side after exiting Mahavira Hall. Purchase prayer beads and blessing bags.
