Madame Tussauds Shanghai Overview

A comprehensive guide to Shanghai's world-famous wax museum.

Madame Tussauds Shanghai is a world-famous wax museum located in the heart of Shanghai. It features lifelike wax figures of international and Chinese celebrities, including movie stars, singers, sports icons, and political figures.

With over 100 wax figures spread across multiple themed zones, visitors can get up close and personal with their favorite stars. The museum uses cutting-edge technology to create incredibly realistic figures that are almost indistinguishable from real people. It's a fun and interactive experience for visitors of all ages.

Recommended Tour Route

Madame Tussauds Shanghai features six exciting themed zones, each offering unique interactive experiences with lifelike wax figures.

Blossoms Shanghai perfectly recreates the old Shanghai atmosphere from the hit drama, where you can take photos with wax figures of Hu Ge (as Bao Zong) and Tiffany Tang (as Miss Wang). Music Dream Factory is a paradise for music fans, featuring Taylor Swift in her tour film costume, along with Wang Jiaer, Hua Chenyu, and Michael Jackson.

Glamour Zone brings together Hollywood A-listers like Leonardo DiCaprio, James Bond, and Benedict Cumberbatch, plus Chinese stars including Bai Jingting and Hou Minghao. Marvel Academy is a must-visit for superhero fans, with Thor, Spider-Man, Captain America, and Hulk, plus interactive games like "Hulk Punch" and free 4D movies.

Three-Body Universe offers an immersive sci-fi experience with wax figures from the Three-Body Problem series and interactive Morse code puzzles. Sports Glory lets you stand next to sports legends like Yao Ming and Kobe Bryant.
