About Shanghai

The Pearl of the Orient

Shanghai, China's economic powerhouse, is a vibrant metropolis where colonial-era architecture stands alongside futuristic skyscrapers. As one of the world's busiest ports, it has been a melting pot of cultures for over a century.

From the historic Bund waterfront to the futuristic skyline of Lujiazui, from traditional gardens to cutting-edge art districts, Shanghai offers an unforgettable blend of old and new, East and West.
