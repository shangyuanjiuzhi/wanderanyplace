Shuyanfu*Shanghai (蜀宴赋*上海)

Hanfu Experience

Recommended

Shuyanfu*Shanghai offers an immersive Tang Dynasty-themed dining experience. You can wear exquisite Hanfu costumes, enjoy Guzheng music and classical dance performances, and savor authentic Sichuan imperial cuisine. Perfect for people who want to experience Hanfu culture and Tang Dynasty palace banquets, as well as food lovers who enjoy traditional cultural performances and exquisite Sichuan cuisine.

Price Range

Average cost per person: ¥560

Address

1/F, Building 38, No.535 Jinqiao Road

Suci Xieyan (苏词蟹宴)

Hanfu Experience

Recommended

Suci Xieyan perfectly combines traditional Hanfu culture with exquisite crab feast. Named after Song Dynasty poet Su Shi, it offers a poetic dining experience where you can wear Hanfu and savor Yangcheng Lake hairy crabs. Perfect for people who love Hanfu culture and want to experience ancient garden dining atmosphere, as well as crab feast lovers and traditional culture enthusiasts.

Price Range

Average cost per person: ¥350

Address

Room 402, No.18 Zhongshan East 1st Road, Huangpu District

Dunhuang Frescoes (敦煌神画)

Dunhuang Theme

Recommended

Dunhuang Frescoes features exquisite reproductions of Dunhuang murals, creating a mysterious Silk Road atmosphere. The dishes blend Central Asian and Chinese cultural characteristics, offering unique flavors and artistic presentations. Perfect for people interested in Dunhuang culture and Silk Road history, as well as food lovers who enjoy creative fusion cuisine and artistic ambiance.

Price Range

Average cost per person: ¥500

Address

Room 501, No.22 Zhongshan East 2nd Road

Tuyuewu (兔月屋)

Rabbit Cafe

Recommended

Tuyuewu is a delightful rabbit-themed cafe where you can interact with cute rabbits while enjoying drinks. The moon-themed cozy decor creates a magical atmosphere. Perfect for people who love small animals and enjoy interacting with rabbits, as well as tourists and locals seeking a healing and relaxing experience.

Price Range

Entry fee (2 hours): ¥45 per person

Address

Front Door, No.96, Lane 39, South Shaanxi Road

Chengyin Jiangjing (古建神画*江景餐厅)

River View

Recommended

Chengyin Jiangjing combines ancient Chinese architecture with breathtaking river views. Located along the Huangpu River, you can dine in a traditional Chinese courtyard while enjoying panoramic views of Shanghai's iconic skyline. Perfect for people who love traditional Chinese architecture and want to enjoy Huangpu River views, as well as diners seeking elegant dining experiences and business banquets.

Price Range

Average cost per person: ¥400

Address

Room 202, 2/F, Roosevelt House, No.27 Zhongshan East 1st Road

Xinsheng Jiutun (新胜九屯*小小铁锅炖)

Northeast Cuisine

Recommended

Xinsheng Jiutun brings Northeast China's signature iron pot stew to Shanghai. Slow-cooked in traditional iron pots, the meat and vegetables are rich in flavor. The atmosphere is warm and rustic, with affordable prices. Perfect for people who love Northeast Chinese cuisine and want to taste authentic iron pot stew, as well as diners who value good value and seek warm home-style flavors.

Price Range

Average cost per person: ¥80

Address

No.124-7 Kerong Road, Yuehai Street (opposite Starbucks)

Jichangxing (寄畅兴*百年蟹黄面)

Yu Garden

Recommended

Jichangxing is famous for its century-old crab roe noodles. Located in Yu Garden area, inspired by the ancient "Qu Shui Liu Shang" tradition, the signature crab roe noodles are made with fresh Yangcheng Lake crab roe, golden and rich. Perfect for people who love traditional Shanghai cuisine and want to taste authentic crab roe noodles, as well as tourists visiting Yu Garden who want to experience special dining.

Price Range

Average cost per person: ¥80

Address

3/F, Tianyu Building, Yu Garden Department Store, No.159 Jiuxiaochang Road

Creative Chinese

Bund View

Introduction

Shanghai Cuisine is a delightful Shanghai cuisine restaurant offering authentic family-style Shanghai dishes.
