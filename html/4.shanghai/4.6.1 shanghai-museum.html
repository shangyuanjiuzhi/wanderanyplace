Shanghai Museum Overview

One of China's most important museums, located in People's Square. Its impressive collection includes ancient bronzes, ceramics, calligraphy, paintings, and jade artifacts spanning thousands of years of Chinese history.

Shanghai Museum is one of China's most important cultural institutions, located in the heart of People's Square. This world-class museum houses an extraordinary collection of over 120,000 artifacts that span over 5,000 years of Chinese civilization.

What makes Shanghai Museum special is its remarkable collection. The museum is particularly renowned for its ancient bronzes, considered among the finest in the world, as well as its extensive holdings of ceramics, calligraphy, paintings, and jade artifacts. It's a treasure trove for anyone interested in Chinese history and art.

  • 1F: Olmec, Teotihuacan & Maya Galleries - Birthplace of Mesoamerican civilization
  • 2F: Aztec Gallery - The last empire of Mexico's Central Plateau
