Shanghai Attractions

Discover the most amazing attractions in Shanghai, from iconic landmarks to cultural gems

1

Bund & International Buildings

★★★★★4.9

Shanghai's most iconic waterfront, lined with 52 historic buildings from the colonial era representing various architectural styles including Gothic, Baroque, and Renaissance. Offers stunning views of the Pudong skyline across the Huangpu River.

2-3 hours Huangpu District Free
Learn More
2

Lujiazui Skyline

★★★★★4.8

Home to Shanghai's three iconic skyscrapers: Shanghai Tower (632m, tallest in China), Shanghai World Financial Center (492m, "bottle opener"), and Jinmao Tower (421m, pagoda-inspired). These "Three Musketeers" form one of the most impressive skylines in the world.

3-4 hours Pudong District ¥120-220/person
Learn More
3

Oriental Pearl TV Tower

★★★★☆4.7

Standing at 468 meters, the Oriental Pearl was once China's tallest building. Its distinctive design with 11 spheres of varying sizes makes it one of Shanghai's most recognizable landmarks. The tower offers multiple observation levels, including a transparent glass floor.

2-3 hours Pudong District ¥199/person
Learn More
1

Waibaidu Bridge

★★★★☆4.4

Shanghai's first steel bridge, built in 1907. This historic truss bridge crossing Suzhou Creek has become an iconic landmark. Beautifully illuminated at night, offering excellent views of both the Bund and the skyline.

30-60 minutes Huangpu District Free
Learn More
2

Waitanyuan (Bund Origin)

★★★★☆4.5

The birthplace of modern Shanghai, Waitanyuan features some of the city's earliest Western-style buildings including the British Consulate and the Astor House Hotel. It's a well-preserved area that showcases Shanghai's colonial history.

1-2 hours Huangpu District Free
Learn More
3

Wukang Building

★★★★☆4.5

Formerly Normandie Apartments, this famous Art Deco building at the intersection of Wukang Road and Huaihai Middle Road features a distinctive curved facade and French-inspired design, making it one of Shanghai's most photographed landmarks.

30-60 minutes Xuhui District Free
Learn More
4

Rockbund Art Museum

★★★★☆4.4

Housed in a historic building that was once the Royal Asiatic Society, the Rockbund Art Museum showcases contemporary art exhibitions. Its elegant colonial architecture blends perfectly with modern art installations.

1-2 hours Huangpu District Free
Learn More
5

Sinan Mansions

★★★★☆4.6

A collection of elegant colonial-era villas and gardens. Many famous historical figures once lived here. Today, it's a cultural and commercial center with upscale boutiques, restaurants, and art galleries.

2-3 hours Xuhui District Free
Learn More
6

1933 Laochangfang

★★★★☆4.5

Built in 1933 as a slaughterhouse, this historic industrial building has been transformed into a trendy cultural hub. Its unique architectural style with exposed brick walls and steel beams attracts artists and photographers.

2-3 hours Hongkou District Free
Learn More
7

Xujiahui Cathedral

★★★★☆4.6

Also known as the Cathedral of St. Ignatius, this stunning neo-Gothic church features red brick facade, twin spires reaching 57 meters, and beautiful stained glass windows. One of the finest examples of Gothic architecture in East Asia.

1-2 hours Xuhui District Free
Learn More
1

Shanghai Disneyland

★★★★★4.8

The first Disney resort in Mainland China, featuring Enchanted Storybook Castle - the tallest Disney castle in the world. Offers magical experiences for families with thrilling rides, character meet-and-greets, and spectacular fireworks shows.

1 day Pudong District ¥419-719/person
Learn More
2

Madame Tussauds Shanghai

★★★★☆4.5

Get up close with lifelike wax figures of celebrities, world leaders, and sports stars. Features interactive exhibits and photo opportunities. Located in the heart of Shanghai near Nanjing Road.

1-2 hours Huangpu District ¥200/person
Learn More
3

Shanghai Film Park

★★★★☆4.3

A movie studio and theme park recreating old Shanghai's streets and buildings from the 1930s-1940s. Many famous Chinese films and TV dramas are filmed here. Visitors can watch live performances and experience film-making.

2-3 hours Songjiang District ¥80/person
Learn More
1

Tianzifang

★★★★☆4.6

A trendy arts and crafts district in the former French Concession. Its narrow lanes are filled with art galleries, boutique shops, cafes, and traditional shikumen houses. A perfect place for exploring and finding unique souvenirs.

2-3 hours Xuhui District Free
Learn More
2

Thames Town

★★★★☆4.4

A charming British-style town with cobblestone streets, Tudor-style houses, and a beautiful church. Feels like stepping into a real English village. Popular for wedding photography and leisurely strolls.

2-3 hours Songjiang District Free
Learn More
3

Qibao Old Street

★★★★☆4.6

A well-preserved ancient town with over 1,000 years of history. Features traditional Chinese architecture, winding canals, and a wide variety of local snacks and handicrafts. Comes alive at night with beautiful lanterns.

2-3 hours Minhang District Free
Learn More
4

Sweet Love Road

★★★★☆4.5

Shanghai's most romantic street, lined with love poems on its walls and featuring a mailbox that sends love letters with a special postmark. Couples from all over come here to express their love.

1-2 hours Hongkou District Free
Learn More
5

Dishui Lake

★★★★☆4.2

Shanghai's largest artificial lake, located in Lingang New City. This beautiful lake features a central island and is surrounded by parks, walking paths, and scenic viewpoints. It's a perfect place for cycling, picnicking, and enjoying nature.

Half Day Pudong New Area Free
Learn More
1

Nanjing Road

★★★★☆4.5

One of the world's busiest shopping streets, stretching from the Bund to Jing'an Temple. Features major department stores, international brands, and local specialty shops. Perfect for experiencing Shanghai's vibrant commercial atmosphere.

2-3 hours Huangpu District Free
Learn More
2

West Nanjing Road

★★★★☆4.6

Shanghai's premier luxury shopping district, home to high-end malls like Plaza 66, CITIC Plaza, and Hong Kong Plaza. Features international luxury brands and upscale dining options.

2-3 hours Jing'an District Free
Learn More
3

Huaihai Road & Xintiandi

★★★★☆4.7

Huaihai Road is known as Shanghai's "avenue of fashion," while Xintiandi blends traditional shikumen architecture with modern shopping and entertainment. A trendy area with boutiques, cafes, and nightlife.

2-3 hours Huangpu/Xuhui District Free
Learn More
4

Lujiazui Shopping District

★★★★☆4.6

Home to luxury malls including IFC Mall, Super Brand Mall, and Shanghai Tower Shopping Mall. Offers international luxury brands with stunning views of the Huangpu River and the Bund.

2-3 hours Pudong District Free
Learn More
1

Shanghai Museum

★★★★★4.8

One of China's most important museums, located in People's Square. Its impressive collection includes ancient bronzes, ceramics, calligraphy, paintings, and jade artifacts spanning thousands of years of Chinese history.

2-3 hours Huangpu District Free
Learn More
2

China Art Palace

★★★★☆4.6

Housed in the former China Pavilion from Expo 2010, this massive art museum showcases Chinese contemporary art. Its distinctive red architecture and vast exhibition space make it a must-visit for art lovers.

2-3 hours Pudong District Free
Learn More
3

Power Station of Art

★★★★☆4.5

Shanghai's first public contemporary art museum, housed in a former power station. The massive industrial space is perfect for large-scale installations. It also hosts the Shanghai Biennale every two years.

2-3 hours Huangpu District Free
Learn More
4

Guangfulin Cultural Heritage

★★★★☆4.5

A major archaeological site with 5,000 years of history. Features underwater museum buildings that appear to float on the lake, showcasing Shanghai's earliest human settlements and cultural development.

3-4 hours Songjiang District ¥70/person
Learn More
1

Yu Garden

★★★★☆4.7

A classical Chinese garden built during the Ming Dynasty. Features exquisite rockeries, tranquil ponds, pavilions, and traditional architecture. During Chinese New Year, it hosts a spectacular lantern exhibition.

1-2 hours Huangpu District ¥40/person
Learn More
2

City God Temple

★★★★☆4.5

A bustling temple complex dedicated to Shanghai's City God. The surrounding area features traditional architecture, local snack shops, and souvenir stalls. A great place to experience traditional Chinese culture.

1-2 hours Huangpu District Free
Learn More
3

Jing An Temple

★★★★☆4.5

One of Shanghai's most famous Buddhist temples, located in the heart of Jing'an District. Features a stunning golden pagoda and beautiful traditional architecture, contrasting with the surrounding modern skyscrapers.

1-2 hours Jing'an District ¥50/person
Learn More
