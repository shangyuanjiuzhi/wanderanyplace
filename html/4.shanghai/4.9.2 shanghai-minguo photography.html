Republican-era Photography in Shanghai

Experience Shanghai's glamorous 1930s era and capture stunning photos at iconic historical landmarks

About Republican-era Photography

The Republican era (1912-1949) was a golden age for Shanghai, known as the "Paris of the East." With its Art Deco architecture, elegant qipao dresses, and sophisticated lifestyle, Shanghai became a symbol of modernity and glamour.

Today, Republican-era photography has become a popular trend, allowing visitors to step back in time and experience the elegance of 1930s Shanghai. Many studios offer complete packages including qipao rental, period makeup, and guided photo sessions at historic locations.

Whether you're interested in capturing the city's colonial heritage or recreating scenes from classic Chinese films, Shanghai offers endless opportunities for stunning Republican-era photography.
