Thames Town Overview

A comprehensive guide to Shanghai's unique British-style town, featuring cobblestone streets, Tudor-style houses, and a beautiful church.

Thames Town is a unique British-style residential and commercial development located in Songjiang District. Built to replicate a traditional English village, it features cobblestone streets, Tudor-style architecture, red telephone boxes, and a beautiful Gothic-style church.

The town feels like stepping into a real English village, with its charming storefronts, tea houses, and flower-lined streets. It has become a popular destination for wedding photography, leisurely strolls, and experiencing a taste of England without leaving Shanghai. The peaceful atmosphere and distinctive architecture make it a delightful place to spend an afternoon.

Recommended Tour Route

Start your visit at the Thames Church, the soul landmark of the town. This Gothic-style church is the absolute center, offering picturesque views from every angle. The lawn and stone paths in front are classic photo spots, and it's a popular wedding photography location.

Nearby is Zhongshuge Bookstore, one of Shanghai's most beautiful bookstores. Its mirror and glass design creates an immersive "sea of books" experience. Enjoy a coffee and spend a peaceful afternoon there. Hours: Tue-Sun 9:00-18:00 (Mon 11:00-18:00)

Visit YOUNG Art Museum, a new cultural landmark known for its stunning red spiral staircase. It hosts contemporary art exhibitions and cross-disciplinary events. Free admission, open 10:00-16:30 (closed Monday)

Stroll along the Huating Lake waterfront area on the east side. With its yacht marina and lakeside promenade, it offers serene views and is a perfect spot for capturing the church's full panorama.

Don't just stick to the main roads. Explore Hayes Street, Kent Street, and Soho Street for colorful British terraced houses, vintage street lamps, and charming courtyards - every corner holds a surprise.
