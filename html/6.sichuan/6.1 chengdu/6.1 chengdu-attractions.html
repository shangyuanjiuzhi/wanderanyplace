Chengdu Attractions

Discover the most amazing attractions in Chengdu, from giant pandas and ancient temples to vibrant streets and world-class museums

1

Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding

★★★★★ 4.9

The world's most famous panda conservation and research center, home to dozens of giant pandas and red pandas. Arrive early in the morning to watch cubs feeding and playing before the crowds build up.

3-4 hours Chenghua District ¥55/person
Learn More
2

Dujiangyan China Giant Panda Garden

★★★★☆ 4.7

Closed since 23 April 2026 for renovation and upgrading, per the official notice. The reopening date has not been announced — check before you plan a trip out here.

2-3 hours Dujiangyan ¥55/person
Learn More
Closed for Renovation
3

Panda Valley

★★★★☆ 4.6

A semi-wild panda reserve set in bamboo forest, focused on reintroduction training. Forest trails lead to near-natural habitats where pandas climb, nap and forage.

2-3 hours Dujiangyan ¥55/person
Learn More
1

Kuanzhai Alley

★★★★☆ 4.6

Three parallel Qing-dynasty alleys turned into Chengdu's most stylish cultural block. Teahouses, snack stalls, craft shops and courtyard restaurants fill the lanes.

1-2 hours Qingyang District Free
Learn More
2

Jinli Ancient Street

★★★★☆ 4.5

A lively recreation of a Qing-era street beside Wuhou Shrine. Snack vendors, red lanterns, teahouses and Sichuan opera masks make it a classic photo stop.

1-2 hours Wuhou District Free
Learn More
3

Chunxi Road

★★★★☆ 4.5

Chengdu's busiest shopping street, lined with malls, flagship stores and street-food counters. Come in the evening when the pedestrian district lights up.

1-2 hours Jinjiang District Free
Learn More
4

Eastern Suburb Memory

★★★★☆ 4.4

A former factory complex reborn as an industrial-chic arts district. Photo spots, galleries, live music venues and weekend markets fill the old workshops.

2-3 hours Chenghua District Free
Learn More
1

People's Park

★★★★☆ 4.6

The city's living room: teahouses on the lake, bamboo groves, and locals dancing, playing mahjong and matching marriage ads. Order a bowl of tea and slow down.

1-2 hours Qingyang District Free
Learn More
1

Wuhou Shrine

★★★★☆ 4.6

China's largest museum complex honouring Zhuge Liang and the Three Kingdoms era. Historic halls, red walls and the neighbouring Jinli street make it a half-day visit.

2-3 hours Wuhou District ¥50/person
Learn More
2

Mount Qingcheng

★★★★★ 4.7

The birthplace of Taoism and a UNESCO World Heritage site. Forested trails climb past ancient temples, with a cable car available for the summit.

4-5 hours Dujiangyan ¥80/person
Learn More
3

Wenshu Monastery

★★★★☆ 4.5

Chengdu's best-preserved Buddhist monastery, active since the Tang dynasty. Incense halls, a peaceful garden and a famous vegetarian restaurant sit inside the walls.

1-2 hours Qingyang District Free
Learn More
1

Chengdu Museum

★★★★★ 4.7

A modern museum telling Chengdu's 2,300-year story, from Shu culture and shadow puppetry to folk customs. The upper floors offer rooftop views over Tianfu Square.

2-3 hours Qingyang District Free
Learn More
2

Du Fu Thatched Cottage Museum

★★★★☆ 4.6

The thatched cottage where Tang poet Du Fu lived and wrote more than 240 poems. Bamboo gardens, stone steles and a serene classical layout reward a slow visit.

2-3 hours Qingyang District ¥50/person
Learn More
3

Jinsha Site Museum

★★★★★ 4.7

Closed for renovation and upgrading until further notice. The gold Sun Bird disc and the Shu kingdom excavation pits are off view for now — check the official notice before you plan a trip out here.

2-3 hours Qingyang District ¥70/person
Learn More
Closed for Renovation
1

Dujiangyan Irrigation System

★★★★★ 4.8

A 2,200-year-old irrigation project that still waters the Chengdu Plain, and a UNESCO World Heritage site. Walk the Fish Mouth levee, the Anlan suspension bridge and Erwang Temple.

3-4 hours Dujiangyan ¥80/person
