About Xi'an

The Ancient Capital

Xi'an, formerly known as Chang'an, is one of the Four Great Ancient Capitals of China. As the eastern terminus of the Silk Road, it served as the capital for 13 dynasties over more than 3,100 years, including the powerful Qin, Han, and Tang empires.

From the world-famous Terracotta Army to the majestic Ming Dynasty City Wall, from the vibrant Muslim Quarter to the Tang Dynasty cultural shows, Xi'an offers an unparalleled journey through the depths of Chinese history and culture.

Xi'an Zhanguopao (Warring States Robe)

Step into the romance of the Warring States period with a traditional Zhanguopao (战国袍) photoshoot at Xi'an's most iconic historical landmarks

Zhanguopao (战国袍) is a formal robe from China's Warring States period (475-221 BC), featuring wide sleeves, layered curved collars, and rich embroidery. In Xi'an, visitors can rent full outfits (robe, hair, makeup) for professional photo shoots at iconic Tang-era landmarks. Packages run 1-3 hours and cost ¥299-1999. Book Now →
