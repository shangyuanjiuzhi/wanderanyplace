Tang Paradise Overview

A comprehensive guide to Tang Paradise, a spectacular Tang Dynasty-themed cultural park.

Tang Paradise (大唐芙蓉园) is a large-scale Tang Dynasty-themed cultural park built around the original site of Emperor Xuanzong's former palace in the Qujiang District. The park recreates the splendor of the Tang Dynasty imperial gardens with beautiful lake scenery, historic architecture, cultural performances, and the spectacular Dream Back to Tang Dynasty show.

Covering 1,000 mu (about 165 acres), the park features numerous reconstructed Tang Dynasty buildings, pavilions, bridges, and gardens centered around a large lake. Key attractions include the Ziyun Tower, Fengming Nine-Day Theater, and the beautiful gardens that change with the seasons. The park is especially magical in the evening when it is illuminated with thousands of lights and hosts the spectacular water show.

Recommended Visit Route

Tang Paradise is large — follow this route to cover all core highlights without backtracking:

  • Starting point: Enter via the West Gate (Yuyuan Gate) — the most convenient entrance, directly accessible by Metro Line 4.
  • Stop 1: Ziyun Tower (紫云楼). The park's iconic landmark — be sure to climb up for a panoramic view of Furong Lake and the entire park.
  • Stop 2: Tang Poetry Gorge (唐诗峡). Features stone-carved Tang Dynasty poetry — a great spot to soak in the cultural atmosphere.
  • Stop 3: Caixia Corridor (彩霞长廊). Built over the lake, it's a popular spot for Hanfu photography, especially beautiful at night when lit up.
  • Stop 4: Ladies' Pavilion (仕女馆). Learn about the lifestyle of Tang Dynasty women, with performances nearby.
  • Ending: After completing the loop, choose between enjoying the night view at Chang'an Pier or heading out.

Must-See Performances

The park's signature performances bring Tang Dynasty history to life. Both are unforgettable experiences:

  • Dream Back to Tang Dynasty (梦回大唐): For more details, see the dedicated page.
  • Tang Dynasty Dream (大唐追梦): A water-screen light-and-shadow show watched from Tang-style pleasure boats, lasting 50 minutes. With Furong Lake as the stage and Ziyun Tower as the backdrop, the experience is truly unique.

Tips: Arrive around 4:00 PM — you'll catch the daytime garden scenery and seamlessly transition to the dreamy lit-up night scene, making it the most time-efficient and cost-effective visit.
