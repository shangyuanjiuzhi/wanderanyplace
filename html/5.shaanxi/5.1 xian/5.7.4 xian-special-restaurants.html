Xi'an Special Restaurants

Discover Xi'an's most immersive dining experiences - from Tang Dynasty banquets to authentic Chang'an cuisine

Bell Tower Red Bean Citrus Cake

Yuanwei Chang'an (原味长安)

Tang Dynasty Cuisine

Recommended

Yuanwei Chang'an is renowned for its creative, landmark-themed Xi'an signature dishes. Must-tries include Punk QQ Terracotta Warriors — a playful, Instagram-worthy terracotta warrior-shaped dessert with a crunchy punk-style exterior; the refreshing Giant Wild Goose Pagoda Drink, a beautiful layered beverage crafted to mirror the iconic pagoda; and the sweet, nostalgic Bell Tower Red Bean Citrus Cake, which takes inspiration from Xi'an's landmark clock tower. The restaurant's elegant interior features Tang murals, silk lanterns, and traditional wooden furniture, making it perfect for food lovers and cultural travelers seeking creative Xi'an specialties.

Price Range

Average cost per person: ¥220

Address

4F, Gate 2, Xi'an MixC, No.250 Yanzhan Road, Yanta District, Xi'an

Chang'an Da Pai Dang - Ambiance

Six Small Bowls of Guanzhong

Mao Bi Su & Feizi Xiao

Signature Stall & Food Display

Chang'an Da Pai Dang (长安大排档)

Food Stall Experience

Recommended

Chang'an Da Pai Dang is a bustling, Tang Dynasty-themed food hall that brings together all of Xi'an's iconic signature street food under one lively roof. The three must-order signature dishes are Six Small Bowls of Guanzhong — six mini-bowls featuring classic Guanzhong home-cooked stews, meats, and pickled vegetables; Feizi Xiao (Smile of the Concubine), a delicate, sweet, jasmine-infused pastry shaped like a smiling lychee; and Mao Bi Su (Brush-pen Pastry), an ingenious dessert shaped like a traditional Chinese calligraphy brush that you dip into sweet "ink." Perfect for families and groups who want variety and a lively, authentic Tang atmosphere.

Price Range

Average cost per person: ¥80

Address

Building B, Yintai City, No.410 Yanta South Road, Yanta District, Xi'an

Tang Yuyin - Grand Ambiance

Museum Atmosphere

Integrated Museum Exhibits

Private Dining Rooms

Tang Dynasty Art & Decor

Elegant Dining Setting

Cultural Performance Space

Tang Yuyin (唐毓引)

Museum Dining

Recommended

Tang Yuyin offers a one-of-a-kind dining experience built inside a Tang Dynasty-themed museum space — patrons walk through ancient-style exhibits, pottery displays, and Tang murals before sitting down to eat. The spectacular interior features silk curtains, authentic temple-style woodwork, Tang Dynasty pottery and relics on display, and live guzheng and pipa performances that accompany each course. With its museum-worthy ambiance, private VIP dining rooms, and refined Shaanxi imperial cuisine, it is the perfect choice for couples, culture lovers, and anyone who wants a truly unforgettable, multi-sensory Tang Dynasty experience.

Price Range

Average cost per person: ¥320

Address

No.99 Furong West Road, Qujiang New District, Xi'an

Tang An Xuyan - Grand Banquet Hall

Tang Court Dance During Dinner

Live Singing & Dance Performance

Banquet Dining with Performers

Gold-accented Imperial Interior

Tang An Xuyan (唐安序宴)

Singing & Dining

Recommended

Tang An Xuyan offers the ultimate dinner with live song and dance performances experience in Xi'an. A dedicated central stage hosts continuous Tang Dynasty court performances — silk-clad dancers performing the Dance of the Rainbow Skirt, classical pipa players, guzheng concerts, Tang poetry narration, and live opera singing — all while patrons enjoy a multi-course royal banquet. The interior is lavishly decorated with gold accents, throne-style seating, and authentic imperial table settings. Perfect for special occasions, VIP guests, and anyone seeking the most luxurious, immersive Tang Dynasty palace song-and-dance experience in Xi'an.

Price Range

Average cost per person: ¥500

Address

Next to Exit D1, Qiaozikou Station, West Main Street, Lianhu District, Xi'an
