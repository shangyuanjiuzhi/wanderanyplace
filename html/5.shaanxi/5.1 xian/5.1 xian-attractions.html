Xi'an Attractions

Discover the most amazing attractions in Xi'an, from ancient relics to vibrant cultural experiences

1

Terracotta Army

★★★★☆ 4.9

The Eighth Wonder of the World, an army of over 8,000 life-sized clay soldiers and horses guarding the tomb of Emperor Qin Shi Huang. A UNESCO World Heritage site and one of China's most iconic archaeological discoveries.

3-4 hours Lintong District ¥120/person
Learn More
2

Xi'an City Wall

★★★★☆ 4.7

The best-preserved ancient city wall in China, stretching 13.74 kilometers around the old city. Built during the Ming Dynasty, it offers spectacular views and a unique opportunity to cycle or walk along the historic ramparts.

2-3 hours Chengxin Road ¥54/person
Learn More
3

Huaqing Palace

★★★★☆ 4.5

A legendary hot spring palace complex dating back to the Zhou Dynasty, famously associated with the love story of Emperor Xuanzong and Yang Guifei. Features beautiful gardens, ancient architecture, and the stunning Song of Everlasting Sorrow performance.

3-4 hours Lintong District ¥120/person
Learn More
4

Da Tang Bu Ye Cheng

★★★★☆ 4.8

A vibrant pedestrian street that recreates the grandeur of the Tang Dynasty, featuring illuminated architecture, street performances, cultural shows, and immersive experiences that bring ancient Chang'an to life.

2-3 hours Qujiang District Free admission
Learn More
1

The Song of Everlasting Sorrow

★★★★☆ 4.9

A spectacular outdoor performance at Huaqing Palace retelling the legendary love story of Emperor Xuanzong and Yang Guifei, featuring hundreds of performers, stunning costumes, and breathtaking special effects against the backdrop of the Li Mountain.

1.5 hours Huaqing Palace ¥258-988/person
Learn More
2

Dream Back to Tang Dynasty

★★★★☆ 4.6

An immersive multimedia show at the Tang Paradise theme park that transports audiences back to the golden age of the Tang Dynasty, featuring grand palace scenes, dance performances, and cultural storytelling.

1.5 hours Tang Paradise ¥298/person
Learn More
3

Xi'an Ever Love Scene

★★★★☆ 4.5

A large-scale cultural theme park and performance show by the Songcheng Group, featuring the epic Ever Love Scene performance that tells the story of Xi'an's history through stunning visual effects, acrobatics, and musical performances.

3-4 hours Weiyang District ¥310/person
Learn More
4

Great Qin

★★★★☆ 4.5

A thrilling large-scale performance that brings to life the rise and fall of the Qin Dynasty, featuring epic battle scenes, imperial ceremonies, and breathtaking acrobatics in a state-of-the-art theater setting.

1.5 hours Qujiang District ¥258-688/person
Learn More
1

Giant Wild Goose Pagoda

★★★★☆ 4.5

A Buddhist pagoda built in 652 AD to store sutras brought back from India by the monk Xuanzang. Standing 64.5 meters tall, it's one of the most iconic landmarks of Xi'an and a masterpiece of Tang Dynasty architecture.

1-2 hours Yanta District ¥50/person
Learn More
2

Small Wild Goose Pagoda

★★★★☆ 4.3

A lesser-known but equally charming pagoda built in 709 AD, famous for its miraculous ability to self-heal after earthquakes. The pagoda and its surrounding Jianfu Temple offer a peaceful atmosphere away from the crowds.

1-2 hours Beilin District ¥30/person
Learn More
3

Daming Palace Heritage Park

★★★★☆ 4.5

The ruins of the grandest palace complex in Chinese history, covering 3.2 square kilometers. Once the imperial seat of power for 17 Tang Dynasty emperors, it now features archaeological exhibits and reconstructed buildings showcasing its former glory.

2-3 hours Taihua Road ¥60/person
Learn More
4

Xi'an Bell Tower

★★★★☆ 4.5

Built in 1384 during the Ming Dynasty, this iconic tower stands at the exact geographic center of Xi'an. Originally used to strike the hour and warn of danger, it now houses cultural relics and offers panoramic views of the city.

1-2 hours Central Xi'an ¥35/person
Learn More
1

Muslim Quarter (Hui Min Jie)

★★★★☆ 4.5

Xi'an's most famous food street, offering a vibrant mix of Islamic and Chinese culinary traditions. From roujiamo (meat sandwiches) to yangrou paomo (lamb soup with bread), it's a paradise for food lovers exploring authentic Xi'an flavors.

2-3 hours Lianhu District Free admission
Learn More
2

Gao Family Courtyard

★★★★☆ 4.4

A well-preserved traditional courtyard home from the Ming Dynasty, offering a fascinating glimpse into the lifestyle of wealthy Xi'an families. Features exquisite wood carvings, brick decorations, and authentic period furnishings.

1-2 hours Beilin District ¥15/person
Learn More
3

Yongxing Square

★★★★☆ 4.5

A cultural food street showcasing traditional Shaanxi cuisine in a Ming Dynasty-style setting. Famous for its food to remember (摔碗酒) tradition, folk performances, and diverse regional food stalls from across the province.

2-3 hours Xincheng District Free admission
Learn More
4

Tang Paradise

★★★★☆ 4.6

A large-scale Tang Dynasty-themed cultural park built around the original site of Emperor Xuanzong's former palace. Features beautiful lake scenery, historic architecture, cultural performances, and the spectacular Dream Back to Tang Dynasty show.

3-4 hours Qujiang District ¥120/person
Learn More
5

Chang'an Twelve Hours

★★★★☆ 4.6

An immersive themed district inspired by the hit TV series The Longest Day in Chang'an, featuring interactive experiences, traditional performances, and a chance to experience life in the Tang Dynasty capital during its most critical day.

2-3 hours Qujiang District Free admission
Learn More
1

Shaanxi History Museum

★★★★☆ 4.8

One of China's most important history museums, housing over 170,000 artifacts spanning 115,000 years, including precious relics from the Tang Dynasty, the Terracotta Army, and Silk Road treasures. A must-visit for understanding Chinese civilization.

3-4 hours Yanta District Free (reservation required)
Learn More
2

Xi'an Museum

★★★★☆ 4.5

A modern museum showcasing the history of Xi'an through multimedia exhibits and archaeological artifacts. Features interactive displays covering the city's evolution from ancient Chang'an to the modern metropolis.

2-3 hours Qujiang District Free (reservation required)
Learn More
3

Xi'an Beilin Museum

★★★★☆ 4.5

The earliest collection of stele rubbings in China, featuring over 1,000 stone tablets from the Han Dynasty onwards. Famous for the Stele of Nestorianism and the classic Xi'an Beilin (Forest of Steles) collection of calligraphy masterpieces.

2 hours Beilin District ¥30/person
Learn More
1

Zhongnan Mountain

★★★★☆ 4.6

A famous mountain range south of Xi'an, renowned as a retreat for hermits and sages throughout Chinese history. Offers excellent hiking trails, stunning mountain scenery, ancient temples, and the famous Louguantai Taoist Temple where Laozi is said to have written the Tao Te Ching.

4-6 hours Zhouzhi District Free
Learn More
