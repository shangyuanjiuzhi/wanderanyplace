Discover Shangluo

Shaanxi's greenest gem — 20 km of deep gorges, the Qinling's eastern high peaks, and an ancient town where one street crosses two dynasties

About Shangluo

The Green Frontier Between Qinling and Dabashan

Shangluo, nestled between the eastern Qinling and the Dabashan ranges in southern Shaanxi, is the province's most forested prefecture — 67% of its land is woodland. Carved by the Han River and its tributaries, the region blends the rugged north with the misty south, producing deep limestone gorges, alpine meadows, and water towns that evoke Jiangnan rather than the Guanzhong plain.

The Golden Silk Canyon in Shangnan County is Shaanxi's longest and most dramatic gorge, with boardwalks, glass platforms, and turquoise pools cooled by mountain streams even in July. Niubeiliang, at 2,802 m the highest peak of the eastern Qinling, protects one of China's last remaining habitats of the golden takin. And in Danfeng County, Dihua Ancient Town preserves the "one street, two dynasties" Song-Jin boundary, plus the hometown of writer Jia Pingwa, whose novel Qin Opera is set here.

Top Attractions

Explore Shangluo's iconic landmarks — from a 20 km geological wonder of gorges and waterfalls to an alpine peak where golden takin still roam, and a free ancient town split between two medieval dynasties

#1 Attraction

Golden Silk Canyon (金丝大峡谷)

A national 5A scenic area — 20 km of deep V-shaped gorges, vertical cliffs, waterfalls, and turquoise pools carved by the Golden Silk River. Three sections (White Dragon, Black Dragon, Dan River Source) linked by boardwalks and glass platforms. Cool enough to escape summer heat.

#2 Attraction

Niubeiliang National Forest Park (牛背梁国家森林公园)

At 2,802 m, the highest peak of the eastern Qinling — old-growth oak forests, spruce groves, wild azalea meadows, and one of China's best-preserved golden takin habitats. Choose between the easy 3.7 km plank walk in Takun Valley or the 5-6 hour alpine hike to Camel Peak.

2-6 hours Learn More
#3 Attraction

Dihua Ancient Town (棣花古镇)

A free-entry water town where one street straddles two dynasties — Song-Jin Street's stone centerline divides the Southern Song from the Jin Empire. Summer's Thousand-Mu Lotus Pond blooms, Qingfeng Street inspired Jia Pingwa's novel Qin Opera, and Erlang Temple is the only surviving Jin-dynasty building in Shaanxi.

Sample Itineraries

Plan your perfect Shangluo trip with our recommended itineraries

1-Day Shangluo Highlight

Gorges in the morning, water town in the afternoon

1 Morning: Golden Silk Canyon

  • White Dragon Gorge — gentle start, boardwalk along the river
  • Black Dragon Gorge — "Thread of Sky" and Crescent Gorge
  • Dan River Source — Lock-Dragon & Chain Waterfalls

2 Afternoon: Dihua Ancient Town

  • Song-Jin Street — one stride, two dynasties
  • Erlang Temple — Shaanxi's only Jin-dynasty building
  • Lotus pond boating (Jun-Aug only)

3 Evening

  • Return to Xi'an or overnight in Shangluo city

2-Day Shangluo Deep Dive

Alpine forest, gorges, and a literary water town

1 Day 1: Golden Silk Canyon & Dihua

  • Full day at Golden Silk Canyon — all three gorges
  • Late afternoon transfer to Dihua Ancient Town
  • Overnight in Danfeng County homestay

2 Day 2: Niubeiliang

  • Morning — cable car up to Camel Peak mid-elevation
  • Hike the ridgeline — wild azalea meadows & views
  • Afternoon — hike down via Takun Valley plank walk

Note: Early-morning ranger-guided walks in Niubeiliang offer the best chance of spotting golden takin. Niubeiliang is closed in winter (Dec-Feb).
