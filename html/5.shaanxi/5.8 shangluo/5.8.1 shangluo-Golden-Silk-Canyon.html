Golden Silk Canyon Overview

A comprehensive guide to Golden Silk Canyon (金丝大峡谷) — a national forest park and geological wonder in Shangnan County, Shangluo, where 20 km of deep V-shaped gorges, vertical cliffs, waterfalls, and turquoise pools are connected by boardwalks and suspension bridges.

Golden Silk Canyon in Shangnan County, Shangluo, is one of Shaanxi's most spectacular natural parks — carved by the Golden Silk River over millions of years into a 20 km network of deep gorges with vertical cliffs, waterfalls, and turquoise pools. The canyon is divided into three main sections — White Dragon Gorge, Black Dragon Gorge, and Dan River Source — each with its own distinct character and highlights.

Boardwalks and suspension bridges connect the sections, making the full route accessible to most visitors. The park is a national 5A-level scenic area and a popular summer escape, with cool mountain air even in July and August. Glass viewing platforms and cliff-hanging trails add an extra thrill to the scenery.

Must-See Highlights

Golden Silk Canyon is divided into three main sections, each with its own distinct scenery and character. The boardwalk route connects all three, making it easy to experience the full range of the canyon's geology, waterfalls, and narrow gorge formations in a single visit.

  • 1. White Dragon Gorge (白龙峡) — The Gentle Start: This is the canyon's opening section, with a flat, easy trail along the river. Highlights include Immortal Lake (仙人湖), Horse-Created Spring (马刨泉), Stone-Born Tree (石生树), and the beautiful White Dragon Lake (白龙湖). A perfect warm-up before the deeper gorges ahead.
  • 2. Black Dragon Gorge (黑龙峡) — The Narrowest Thrill: The terrain becomes narrower and more dramatic. This section features the iconic "Thread of Sky" (一线天) — where the canyon walls squeeze so close that only a sliver of sky is visible — and the Crescent Gorge (月牙峡), a curved passage carved by millennia of water flow. The canyon atmosphere here is at its most intense.
  • 3. Dan River Source (丹江源) — Waterfall Central: This is the waterfall hub of the canyon. The Lock-Dragon Waterfall (锁龙瀑布) and Chain Waterfall (连环瀑布) are the star attractions, cascading down the cliff faces into deep turquoise pools. When water flow is heavy, rainbows form in the mist — a photographer's dream.
