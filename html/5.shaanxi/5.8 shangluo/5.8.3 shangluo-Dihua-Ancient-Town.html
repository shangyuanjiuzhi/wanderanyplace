Dihua Ancient Town Overview

A comprehensive guide to Dihua Ancient Town (棣花古镇) — a well-preserved water town on the ancient road between Qin and Chu, where a single street straddles the Southern Song and Jin dynasties, and hometown of writer Jia Pingwa, author of the novel Qin Opera.

Dihua Ancient Town in Shangluo's Danfeng County sits on the old Qin-Chu boundary road, named for the wild hibiscus that once bloomed along its banks. Its claim to fame is the Song-Jin Street, where a carved stone line in the middle of the pavement divides two former states — step across it and you "cross two dynasties in one stride." The town is also the hometown of Jia Pingwa, one of China's most celebrated contemporary writers, whose novel Qin Opera (《秦腔》) draws directly from the town's Qingfeng Street.

Entry is free, making it the easiest add-on to a Golden Silk Canyon or Niubeiliang trip. Summer brings the Thousand-Mu Lotus Pond into full bloom; history lovers come for the Erlang Temple, the only surviving Jin-dynasty building in Shaanxi Province.

Core Highlights

Four sights define Dihua: a vast lotus pond best in summer, a street split between two medieval states, the literary hometown of a Booker-nominated author, and the only Jin-dynasty building left in Shaanxi.

  • 1. Thousand-Mu Lotus Pond (千亩荷塘) — Summer's Best Photo: Best viewed June to August, when the pond fills with blooms. Rent a boat to glide among the flowers — electric boat ¥40/person/30 min, pedal boat ¥30/person/hour, half price for children. Extremely photogenic at dawn and dusk.
  • 2. Song-Jin Street (宋金街) — One Stride, Two Dynasties: An old street divided by a stone centerline — the east side represents the Southern Song, the west the Jin Empire. The architecture on each side follows its respective dynasty's style. Walking the line gives you the surreal feeling of crossing eight centuries of border history in a single step.
  • 3. Qingfeng Street (清风街) — A Novel Come to Life: The real-life prototype of the setting in Jia Pingwa's novel Qin Opera (《秦腔》). Bluestone lanes, old-style shops, and traditional dwellings preserve the everyday atmosphere and literary aura that earned the novel China's Mao Dun Literature Prize.
  • 4. Erlang Temple (二郎庙) — Shaanxi's Only Jin-Dynasty Building: The sole surviving Jin-dynasty (1115-1234) structure in Shaanxi Province, with high historical value and distinctive architecture. A rare chance to see Jin-era timber construction up close.

Detailed Information

Location

Danfeng County, Shangluo, Shaanxi Province

Opening Hours

Open all day (town area)

Boats: 9:00-17:00

Best Time to Visit

June-August for lotus blooms; spring and autumn for quiet strolls.

Tickets

Town entry: Free

Electric boat: ¥40/person/30 min

Pedal boat: ¥30/person/hour (children half price)

Reservation Information

No reservation needed; weekends busier with domestic tour groups

Transportation

Bus from Xi'an to Danfeng (~¥50, 3hrs); town is 10 min from the county center
