Shangluo Attractions

From the deep gorges of Golden Silk Canyon and the alpine forests of Niubeiliang to the charm of Dihua Ancient Town — Shangluo is Shaanxi's greenest gem tucked between the Qinling and Dabashan mountains

1

Golden Silk Canyon (金丝大峡谷)

★★★★★ 4.7

A national forest park and geological wonder carved by the Golden Silk River — 20 km of deep V-shaped gorges, vertical cliffs, waterfalls, and turquoise pools connected by boardwalks and suspension bridges. The best-known section, the "Grand Canyon," features glass platforms and cliff-hanging trails that make it one of Shaanxi's most scenic summer escapes, with cool mountain air even in July and August.

5-6 hours Shangnan County ¥90 + shuttle ¥30

2

Niubeiliang National Forest Park (牛背梁国家森林公园)

★★★★☆ 4.5

At 2,802 m, Niubeiliang is the highest peak in the eastern Qinling — a vast alpine preserve with old-growth oak forests, spruce groves, wild azalea meadows, and one of the best-preserved habitats of the golden takin in China. The main hiking route follows a stream up through forest to the alpine meadow, with cable car access to the mid-elevation zone for less fit visitors. A designated section offers guided nature walks to spot wildlife.

6-8 hours Zhen'an County ¥110 + cable ¥80
Learn More
3

Dihua Ancient Town (棣花古镇)

★★★★☆ 4.3

A well-preserved water town on the banks of the Golden Silk River, named for the wild hibiscus (dihua) that once bloomed along its canals. The old stone streets, covered bridges, and Ming-Qing dynasty courtyard houses have been restored with a light hand, making it a popular day trip for its traditional workshops, local food stalls, and scenic boat rides. Free entry, and ideal for combining with Golden Silk Canyon on a single itinerary.

2-3 hours Shangluo City Free
