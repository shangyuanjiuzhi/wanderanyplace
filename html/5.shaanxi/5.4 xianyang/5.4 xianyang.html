About Xianyang

The Qin-Han Capital City

Xianyang, once the capital of both the Qin and Han dynasties, sits on the fertile Wei River plain just west of Xi'an. It holds an unparalleled concentration of imperial tombs — the Qianling Mausoleum (Emperor Gaozong & Wu Zetian) alone shelters 17 subsidiary tombs, while the Han Changling complex spans a vast area of rolling hills.

Beyond its royal heritage, Xianyang charms visitors with the Xiaoling Museum's jade art peak — the Han Immortal on a Pegasus — the riverside serenity of Xianyang Lake, and Fuyuan Alley's vibrant street food scene where locals queue for 10 Guanzhong specialties under ¥10.

Top Attractions

Explore Xianyang's most iconic landmarks — from sacred imperial mausoleums to vibrant alley food

#1 Attraction

Qianling Mausoleum (乾陵)

The only mausoleum in China containing two emperors — Tang Gaozong and Empress Wu Zetian. Sealed for 1,300 years and never excavated, it features the famous Uncharactered Stele and Sixty-One Foreign Envoys.

3-4 hours Learn More
#2 Attraction

Xianyang Museum (咸阳博物馆)

Home to the Han dynasty's greatest jade masterpiece — the Immortal on a Pegasus — plus 3,000 painted Han terracotta warriors, Warring States gold-inlaid bronzes, and royal gold coins.

2 hours Learn More
#3 Attraction

Fuyuan Alley (福园巷子)

Xianyang's most vibrant food & culture alley — five photogenic spots including a grand archway and red lantern lanes, plus 10 must-eat Guanzhong snacks all under ¥10.
