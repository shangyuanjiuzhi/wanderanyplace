Gudu Corridor Bridge Overview

A comprehensive guide to Gudu Corridor Bridge — a grand Tang Dynasty-style covered wooden bridge spanning the Wei River, recreating the legendary Xianyang Ferry and serving as Xianyang's most photogenic riverside landmark.

Gudu Corridor Bridge (古渡廊桥) is a striking modern recreation of the historic "Xianyang Ferry" — one of the celebrated Eight Views of Guanzhong since the Tang Dynasty. The original ferry crossing was a vital transport hub on the Wei River for over a millennium, where merchants, officials and poets alike paused to admire the river flowing toward Chang'an (modern Xi'an). Today's bridge, built in Tang Dynasty architectural style with soaring wooden eaves and red-painted pillars, spans the Wei River as a pedestrian-only covered corridor.

The bridge connects Xianyang's north bank with its south bank, offering panoramic views of the Xianyang Lake wetland park, the distant Qingwei Tower (清渭楼), and seasonal cherry blossoms that frame the riverbank in spring. By day it is a peaceful promenade; by night the bridge illuminates in warm lantern light, its reflection doubling across the dark Wei River surface. Two spots draw most of the Instagram shots: the bridge's second-floor observation deck, and the nearby "Tree of Life" — a solitary tree beside Xianyang Lake that creates a striking silhouette against the bridge.

Top Instagram Spots

Gudu Corridor Bridge and its immediate surroundings are Xianyang's most viral photo locations. Two spots dominate social media feeds — the Second-Floor Observation Deck for wide-angle views and the Tree of Life for dreamy lakeside silhouettes.

  • 1. Second-Floor Observation Deck — The Panoramic Viewpoint: Ascend to the bridge's second level for the best vantage point in the area. From here you can look down at the Wei River's spring blossoms and wetlands stretching along Xianyang Lake, and catch a distant view of Qingwei Tower (清渭楼) — another Tang Dynasty icon perched on the south bank. Wide-angle lenses work best here; visit at golden hour for the warmest light.
  • 2. Tree of Life — The Viral Silhouette Spot: This solitary tree near the bridge has become Xianyang's most beloved Instagram landmark. Photograph the lake and the bridge through the tree's branches as foreground framing, or compose the tree as a central silhouette with the bridge illuminated behind it. Dramatic backlighting at dusk produces the most striking results — when the setting sun or glowing lanterns outline the tree against the lit-up corridor bridge.
