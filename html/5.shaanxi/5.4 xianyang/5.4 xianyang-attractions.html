Xianyang Attractions

From the untouched imperial tomb of China's only female emperor and Han Dynasty terracotta warriors to riverside walks and snack-filled old alleys — the ancient Qin capital just north of Xi'an

1

Qianling Mausoleum (乾陵)

★★★★☆ 4.7

The joint tomb of Emperor Gaozong and Empress Wu Zetian — China's only mausoleum holding two emperors, and the only Tang imperial tomb never looted. Carved into the granite peak of Liangshan Mountain, it is famed for the mysterious Uncharactered Stele, 61 headless stone statues of foreign envoys, and an underground palace still sealed after 1,300 years.

3-4 hours Qian County ¥100/person

2

Xianyang Museum (咸阳博物馆)

★★★★☆ 4.5

Housed in a Ming Dynasty Confucian temple, this museum's star exhibit is the "Three Thousand Terracotta Warriors" of the Western Han — a palm-sized polychrome army unearthed at Yangjiawan — alongside rare Qin Dynasty artifacts from China's first unified empire. Admission is free with ID.

1-2 hours Weicheng District Free
Learn More
3

Gudu Corridor Bridge (古渡廊桥)

★★★★☆ 4.4

A grand Tang-style covered bridge spanning the Wei River, recreating the ancient "Xianyang Ferry" — once one of the eight famed views of Guanzhong. Stroll the wooden corridors and pavilions by day, and return after dusk when the illuminated bridge reflects across the water.

1 hour Wei River Bank Free
Learn More
4

Fuyuan Alley (福园巷子)

★★★★☆ 4.3

Xianyang's answer to Xi'an's Yongxingfang — a renovated old-street quarter packed with Guanzhong snacks, teahouses, craft shops and night-market stalls. Come hungry: biangbiang noodles, roujiamo and bubble-oven pancakes are all within a hundred meters, and it lights up beautifully after dark.

1-2 hours Qindu District Free
