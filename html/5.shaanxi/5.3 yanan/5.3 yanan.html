About Yan'an

The Sacred Loess Plateau City

Yan'an sits deep in the loess plateau of northern Shaanxi, where dramatic sandstone gorges, sacred imperial mausoleums, and thousand-year-old Buddhist grottoes carve through the ochre landscape. It is home to Hukou — the largest yellow waterfall on Earth — and the Yellow Emperor's Mausoleum, China's most revered ancestral site.

From the slot-canyon light shows of Ganquan Grand Canyon to the silent grandeur of Zhongshan Grottoes' Ten Thousand Buddhas, Yan'an blends natural spectacle with cultural depth. The loess hills also nurture a hearty local cuisine — 洋芋擦擦, 麻辣羊蹄 and 黑白面皮 — rooted in Guanzhong tradition.

Top Attractions

Explore Yan'an's most iconic landmarks — from thunderous waterfalls to sacred mausoleums and hidden grottoes

#1 Attraction

Hukou Waterfall (壶口瀑布)

The largest waterfall on the Yellow River and the world's biggest yellow-hued waterfall — "a thousand miles of river gathered into one teapot spout." Visit in spring for flood surges or winter for surreal ice formations.

#2 Attraction

Yellow Emperor's Mausoleum (黄帝陵)

"The First Mausoleum Under Heaven" — sacred burial site of the Yellow Emperor, legendary ancestor of the Chinese nation. Set among 80,000 ancient cypresses, including one planted 5,000 years ago.

2-3 hours Learn More
#3 Attraction

Ganquan Grand Canyon (甘泉大峡谷)

Known as "China's Antelope Canyon" — hidden red sandstone slot gorges carved by rainwater. Around midday, sunlight beams through fissures painting ochre walls in shifting golds, reds and purples.
