Yan'an Local Food

Discover the rustic, hearty flavors of the Loess Plateau through 4 iconic Yan'an dishes and street snacks

黑白面皮 (Heibai Mianpi Black & White Cold Noodles) Breakfast

Description

A beloved Yan'an breakfast classic, Heibai Mianpi is a striking two-toned cold noodle dish that pairs "black" buckwheat-flour sheets with "white" wheat-flour sheets in a single bowl. The noodles are silky, chewy and springy, tossed with vinegar, garlic water, chili oil and a scatter of cucumber shreds. The contrast of the dark, nutty buckwheat and the smooth wheat sheets — sharp, sour and fiery in one bite — makes it the perfect wake-up call on the Loess Plateau.

Ingredients

1. Buckwheat flour sheets ("black" noodles)
2. Wheat flour sheets ("white" noodles)
3. Chili oil and garlic water
4. Aged vinegar and soy sauce
5. Cucumber shreds and cilantro

Recommended Restaurants

1. 老延安面皮馆 Lao Yan'an Mianpi Guan

2. 二道街小吃 Erdaojie Xiaochi

3. 圣地早点铺 Shengdi Zaodian

和杂面 (Hezamian Mixed-Grain Noodles) Breakfast

Description

Hezamian is a rustic mixed-grain noodle soup that has nourished Yan'an farm families for generations. "和杂" means "blended together" — the dough combines wheat, buckwheat, pea and bean flours, hand-rolled and cut into broad, uneven ribbons. Simmered in a simple broth of tomato, sour pickle juice and fried egg or minced pork, each bowl is hearty, tangy and warming — a genuine taste of Loess Plateau country cooking, best enjoyed steaming-hot in the early morning.

Ingredients

1. Mixed-grain flour (wheat, buckwheat, pea, bean)
2. Hand-rolled noodle ribbons
3. Tomato and sour pickle broth
4. Fried egg or minced pork topping
5. Scallions and chili oil

Recommended Restaurants

1. 老陕北和杂面 Lao Shanbei Hezamian

2. 农家和杂面坊 Nongjia Hezamianfang

3. 宝塔山面馆 Baotashan Mian Guan

麻辣羊蹄 (Malayangti Spicy Lamb Trotters)

Description

Malayangti is Yan'an's most famous street-side indulgence — lamb trotters slow-braised for hours in a broth loaded with Sichuan peppercorns, dried chilies and more than a dozen spices, until the skin turns gloriously gelatinous and falls off the bone. Served steaming hot and eaten with the hands, each bite delivers a numbing, tingling "mala" kick followed by a rich, savory depth. Locals gather around night-market stalls to gnaw on trotters late into the evening — an experience as social as it is delicious.

Ingredients

1. Fresh lamb trotters
2. Sichuan peppercorns and dried chilies
3. Star anise, cinnamon and bay leaves
4. Ginger, garlic and scallions
5. Soy sauce and cooking wine

Recommended Restaurants

1. 延安夜市麻辣羊蹄 Yan'an Night Market Stall

2. 老白羊蹄馆 Lao Bai Yangti Guan

3. 二道街羊蹄摊 Erdaojie Yangti

洋芋擦擦 (Yangyu Caca Grated Potato Strips)

Description

Yangyu Caca — the name comes from the local dialect, where "擦擦" means "to grate" — is a humble yet irresistible potato dish born of the Loess Plateau. Potatoes are grated into strips, coated in flour, and steamed until tender, then stir-fried or served cold with garlic, vinegar, chili oil and scallions. The result is a delightful mix of textures: soft and floury inside with a gently chewy, seasoned coating. Once a frugal farm staple, it is now one of the most beloved everyday dishes across northern Shaanxi.

Ingredients

1. Fresh potatoes, grated into strips
2. Wheat flour coating
3. Garlic, scallions and cilantro
4. Chili oil and black vinegar
5. Sichuan pepper and salt

Recommended Restaurants

1. 陕北人家 Shanbei Renjia

2. 老延安洋芋擦擦 Lao Yan'an Yangyu Caca

3. 黄土情餐馆 Huangtuqing Canting
