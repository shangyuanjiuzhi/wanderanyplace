Hukou Waterfall Overview

A comprehensive guide to Hukou Waterfall, the largest waterfall on the Yellow River and the world's biggest yellow-hued waterfall.

Hukou Waterfall (壶口瀑布), located where the Yellow River narrows from 400 meters to just 30 meters wide, is one of China's most awe-inspiring natural spectacles. The name "Hukou" means "teapot spout" — a thousand miles of the Yellow River's ochre waters are funneled through a narrow gorge, plunging downward in a thundering cascade of spray, rainbows and raw power. It is the second-largest waterfall in China after Huangguoshu, and the only yellow waterfall in the world.

The waterfall straddles the border between Shanxi and Shaanxi provinces — visitors can view it from the Shaanxi side at Yichuan County, Yan'an. Each season transforms the falls: spring brings dramatic flood surges as ice thaws; summer offers the most powerful flow; autumn paints the surrounding cliffs in golden light; and winter creates surreal ice formations as frozen spray coats the cliffs in crystalline white.

Seasonal Highlights

Hukou Waterfall transforms dramatically across the four seasons. The images on the right showcase the waterfall in spring, summer, fall and winter — each offering a completely different experience.

  • Spring (Mar–May) — Thaw Surge: As ice melts upstream, the Yellow River roars back to life with dramatic flood surges. The water volume peaks, sending towering spray columns high above the gorge. This is the most powerful and thunderous time to visit.
  • Summer (Jun–Aug) — Golden Torrent: The waterfall reaches its widest span, with ochre waters cascading across the full 30-meter gorge. Rainbows frequently arch across the spray on sunny mornings. Wear a rain poncho — the mist soaks everything within 50 meters.
  • Fall (Sep–Nov) — Golden Cliffs: Water levels stabilize and the surrounding cliffs glow amber in the low autumn light. The clearer air and softer flow make this the best season for photography, with the waterfall's texture and color at their most photogenic.
  • Winter (Dec–Feb) — Ice Wonderland: Frozen spray coats the cliff walls in crystalline ice sculptures, while the river continues to flow beneath. Smaller icefalls form along the banks. A surreal, otherworldly scene — bring warm layers and non-slip boots.
