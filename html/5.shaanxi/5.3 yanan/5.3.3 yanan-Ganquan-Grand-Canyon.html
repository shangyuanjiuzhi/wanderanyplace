Canyon Overview

A comprehensive guide to Ganquan Grand Canyon — known as "China's Antelope Canyon," a hidden network of narrow red sandstone slot canyons near Yucha Village in Ganquan County.

Ganquan Grand Canyon (甘泉大峡谷), discovered in 2017 near Yucha Village in Ganquan County, Yan'an, is a breathtaking network of narrow slot canyons carved through red sandstone by centuries of rainwater and flash floods. Often called "China's Antelope Canyon," it rivals Arizona's famous slot canyons with its wavy, undulating rock walls and magical midday light beams. When sunlight filters down through the narrow fissures above, the ochre walls glow in shifting shades of gold, crimson and purple — a photographer's dream.

The canyon system consists of several named branches scattered across the loess plateau. Due to weather and conservation needs, the park randomly opens 2–3 canyons each day — meaning no two visits follow the exact same route. But regardless of which branches are open, Huashu Gorge (桦树沟) is always the must-visit centerpiece, the canyon responsible for 90% of the viral photographs that put this place on the map.

Recommended Routes

The park randomly opens 2–3 canyons each day based on weather and conservation needs, so routes are not fixed. But no matter which branches are open, Huashu Gorge is always the must-visit centerpiece. The images on the right show the canyon landscapes across the different branches.

  • Huashu Gorge (桦树沟) — Must-Visit Centerpiece: The visual crown jewel of the entire scenic area. Approximately 1 km long, it features the richest rock-wall textures and the most dramatic light-and-shadow effects. An estimated 90% of the viral photographs circulating online were taken here. Recommended visit time: 1–1.5 hours. Arrive around midday when sunlight beams down through the top fissures for the signature glowing-wall shots.
  • Longba Gorge (龙巴沟) — Wilderness Adventure: The longest and most untamed branch at roughly 2.5 km. Sections require sideways shuffling or shallow wading through water, heightening the sense of exploration. Best suited for visitors in good physical condition who enjoy a more rugged, off-the-beaten-path experience.
  • Mudan Gorge / Yueya Gorge (牡丹沟/月牙沟) — Intimate & Quiet: Relatively short and delicately sculpted, with fewer visitors. Ideal for portrait photography and quiet appreciation of the rock textures — the soft, diffused light and smooth curved walls create a gallery-like atmosphere that rewards patience.
