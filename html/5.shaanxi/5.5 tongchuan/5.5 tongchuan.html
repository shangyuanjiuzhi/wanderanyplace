About Tongchuan

The Ceramic Heart of the Loess Plateau

Tongchuan, sitting on the loess plateau of central Shaanxi north of Xi'an, shelters two treasures of very different kinds: Chenlu Ancient Town — the last living flame of the famed Yaozhou Kiln, where 600 households still make pottery the Song Dynasty way — and Yuhua Palace, the Tang Dynasty royal summer retreat where the revered monk Xuanzang spent his final years translating Buddhist sutras.

Beyond heritage, Tongchuan charms with forested highlands — the Yuhua scenic area's 95% canopy earns it the name "natural oxygen bar," with summer averages near 23°C. Walk terraced cave-dwellings paved with broken pottery shards, watch old potters throw bowls on foot-powered wheels, then climb to the cave where Xuanzang completed his life's work — a quiet, uncrowded counterpoint to Xi'an's Tang sites.

Top Attractions

Explore Tongchuan's two iconic landmarks — from a living kiln village to a Tang emperor's forested summer palace

#1 Attraction

Chenlu Ancient Town (陈炉古镇)

The last surviving fire of the Yaozhou Kiln — a living village where 600 households still make pottery the Song Dynasty way. Walls stacked from broken firing chambers, paths paved with shards, and a century-old family workshop where you can throw your own bowl on a foot-powered wheel.

3-4 hours Learn More
#2 Attraction

Tang Yuhua Palace (唐玉华宫)

A Tang Dynasty royal summer palace where the revered monk Xuanzang spent his final years translating Buddhist sutras. Today the forested valley holds palace foundations, the Sucheng Courtyard ruins where Xuanzang lived and worked, cliff grottoes, and a 95%-canopy "natural oxygen bar."
