Tongchuan Attractions

From a 1,400-year-old living kiln village and a Tang emperor's summer palace to the birthplace of Chinese celadon — Tongchuan is the ceramic and cultural heart of the Shaanxi loess plateau

1

Chenlu Ancient Town (陈炉古镇)

★★★★☆ 4.6

A living kiln village with 1,400 years of continuous ceramic-making — the last surviving fire of the famed Yaozhou Kiln. Crawl through terraced cave-dwellings stacked up the valley walls, walk on paths paved with broken pottery shards, and watch artisans throw bowls by hand exactly as their Song Dynasty ancestors did. The whole town is a museum where the kilns still smoke.

3-4 hours Yin District Free

2

Tang Yuhua Palace (唐玉华宫)

★★★★☆ 4.4

The summer palace of Emperor Taizong of Tang — built in 624 AD as a cool mountain retreat from the Chang'an heat, and later the place where Xuanzang translated Buddhist sutras in his final years. Today the forested valley holds palace foundations, cliff caves, and the serenity that drew Tang China's most revered monk. A quiet, less-crowded alternative to the more famous Tang sites.

2-3 hours Yuhua Town ¥60/person
