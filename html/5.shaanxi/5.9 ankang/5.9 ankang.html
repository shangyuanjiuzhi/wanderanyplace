Discover Ankang

Where the Qinling slopes meet the Han River — a 12,000-mu golden-terrace living museum and "Shaanxi's Thousand-Island Lake"

About Ankang

The "South-of-the-Yangtze" of Shaanxi

Ankang, in southern Shaanxi where the Qinling slopes descend to the Han River, is the province's "south-of-the-Yangtze" pocket — mild climate, abundant water, and the greenest scenery in the region. Two headline sights anchor a visit: the Fengyan Ancient Terraces and Yinghu Lake.

In Hanyin County, the Fengyan Ancient Terraces — over 12,000 mu of stepped paddies carved into the hills from the Qing Qianlong reign by Hunan migrants — turn from mirror water in spring to rolling golden rice waves in autumn, with a ridge viewing platform that catches sunrise and sea of clouds. Southeast of the city, Yinghu Lake, formed by the Ankang Dam on the Han River, is "Shaanxi's Thousand-Island Lake" — 77 km² of calm green water linking Cuiping Island's water-sports hub and the love-themed Jinluo Island crowned by the Luofeng Tower.

Top Attractions

Explore Ankang's iconic sights — from a 12,000-mu golden-terrace living museum to an island-dotted lake dubbed "Shaanxi's Thousand-Island Lake"

#1 Attraction

Fengyan Ancient Terraces (凤堰古梯田景区)

Northern China's largest surviving Qing-dynasty rice terraces — over 12,000 mu of stepped fields carved into the Hanyin hills by Hunan migrant settlers. Spring irrigation turns them into a mirror gallery; autumn gilds the slopes at harvest. The Fengjiang Viewing Platform is the ace spot for sunrise and sea of clouds. Free entry.

#2 Attraction

Yinghu Lake Scenic Area (瀛湖风景区)

A national 4A scenic area on the Han River, dubbed "Shaanxi's Thousand-Island Lake" — 77 km² of calm green water dotted with islands. Boat cruises link Cuiping Island's water-sports hub and the love-themed Jinluo Island crowned by the Luofeng Tower. A relaxed half-day on the water.

Sample Itineraries

Plan your perfect Ankang trip with our recommended itineraries

1-Day Ankang Highlight

Golden terraces in the morning, island cruise in the afternoon

1 Morning: Fengyan Ancient Terraces

  • Fengjiang Viewing Platform — sunrise & sea of clouds
  • Overlook the golden terrace expanse (autumn: Sept–Oct)
  • Village homestay lunch — farm-to-table

2 Afternoon: Yinghu Lake

  • Boat cruise to Cuiping Island — water sports
  • Jinluo Island — Luofeng Tower 360° panorama

3 Evening

  • Ankang city food streets — silver fish specialty

2-Day Ankang Deep Dive

Slow photography on the terraces, then full lake loop

1 Day 1: Fengyan Ancient Terraces

  • Dawn at Fengjiang platform — golden hour shoot
  • Ridge-road walk along the layered contour fields
  • Overnight Hanyin homestay

2 Day 2: Yinghu Lake

  • Full lake cruise — Cuiping Island water sports
  • Jinluo Island — Luofeng Tower & wedding museum
  • Lakeside dinner — silver fish, then return to Xi'an

Note: Terraces are most photogenic in golden autumn (mid-Sept to mid-Oct) or spring mirror-water (May); lake cruises run Apr–Oct.
