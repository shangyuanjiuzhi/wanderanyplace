Yinghu Lake Overview

A comprehensive guide to the Yinghu Lake Scenic Area (瀛湖风景区) — a national 4A scenic area on the Han River, dubbed "Shaanxi's Thousand-Island Lake," with 77 km² of calm green water dotted with islands and backed by the forested Qinling foothills.

Formed by the Ankang Hydropower Dam on the Han River, Yinghu Lake stretches 77 km² across Shaanxi's greenest prefecture. Boat cruises link its two star islands — Cuiping Island, the water-sports hub, and Jinluo Island, a romantic love-themed islet crowned by the Luofeng Tower.

A relaxed half-day on the water — easy to pair with Ankang city's food streets, and the most accessible "Jiangnan-style" lake experience in northern China.

Must-See Islands

Yinghu's two headline islands sit at the heart of the scenic area — one built for water-sport thrills, the other for romance. A cruise between them is the classic Yinghu loop.

  • 1. Cuiping Island (翠屏岛) — Water-Sports Hub: The largest island in Yinghu Lake and its water-sports centre. Thrill-seekers can ride motorboats and the water flying dragon, while families take it easy with kayaks and hand-cranked boats. The on-island Yinghu Hotel has a heated pool — a cool summer retreat.
  • 2. Jinluo Island (金螺岛) — Romantic Love Isle: A love-themed islet. Climb the Luofeng Tower (螺峰塔) for a 360° panorama of the whole lake, and don't miss the wedding-customs museum and wishing pool — both well worth a stroll.

Detailed Information

Location

Southeast of Ankang City, Shaanxi Province

Opening Hours

8:00-17:30

Best Time to Visit

April to October for clear water and mild weather.

Tickets

Entry: ¥70

Boat cruise: extra

Reservation Information

Tickets at the dock; boats run on a fixed cruise schedule, arrive early on weekends

Transportation

Bus from Ankang city to Yinghu Dock (~30 min); then boat to islands
