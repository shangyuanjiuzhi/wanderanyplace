Ankang Attractions

From the Qing-dynasty Fengyan terraces cascading down the Hanyin hills to the island-dotted waters of Yinghu Lake — Ankang is "south-of-the-Yangtze" Shaanxi, where the Qinling slopes meet the Han River

1

Fengyan Ancient Terraces Scenic Area (陕西凤堰古梯田景区)

★★★★★ 4.6

The largest surviving Qing-dynasty terraces in northern China — over 12,000 mu of stepped fields carved into the Hanyin hills from the 1760s by Hunan migrant settlers. Spring irrigation turns them into a mirror gallery (May), summer brings deep green, and autumn gilds the slopes at harvest. A living museum of rice-farming heritage on the old migration route between Shaanxi, Sichuan and Hubei, with village homestays and photo platforms along the ridge roads.

4-6 hours Hanyin County Free

2

Yinghu Lake Scenic Area (瀛湖风景区)

★★★★☆ 4.4

A national 4A scenic area on the Han River, dubbed "Shaanxi's Thousand-Island Lake" — 77 km² of calm green water dotted with islands, backed by the forested Qinling foothills. Boat cruises link Jinluo Island's tea plantations, Cuiiping Island's gardens, and the bird island sanctuary; lakeside restaurants serve the local specialty, silver fish. A relaxed half-day on the water, easy to pair with Ankang city's food streets.

4-5 hours Ankang City ¥70 + boat
