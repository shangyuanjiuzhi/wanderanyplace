Fengyan Ancient Terraces Overview

A comprehensive guide to the Fengyan Ancient Terraces Scenic Area (陕西凤堰古梯田景区) — the largest surviving Qing-dynasty rice terraces in northern China, over 12,000 mu of stepped fields carved into the Hanyin hills from the 1760s by Hunan migrant settlers travelling the old migration route between Shaanxi, Sichuan and Hubei.

Spanning more than 12,000 mu of stepped paddies around Xuanwo Town in Hanyin County, Fengyan is a living museum of rice-farming heritage. Carved out of the hillsides from the Qing Qianlong reign (c. 1760s) by settlers from Hunan who followed the great Huguang-to-Shaanxi migration, the terraces follow the contours of the Feng and Yan family lands — hence the name Feng-yan (凤堰).

The fields shift with the seasons: spring irrigation turns them into a mirror gallery, summer dresses them in deep green, and autumn gilds the slopes at harvest. With free entry, ridge-road photo platforms, and village homestays serving farm-to-table meals, it is one of Shaanxi's most rewarding rural photography destinations.

Best Time to Visit — Autumn Golden Rice

Autumn (mid-to-late September through mid-to-late October) is the unmissable season — it is when the rice ripens and the entire mountainside becomes rolling waves of golden grain. The colour shifts from pale gold to deep amber as harvest progresses, and the terraces' layered curves catch the low autumn sun, making it one of the most photogenic rural landscapes in Shaanxi and an ideal destination for photography.

If you miss the autumn window, the next-best window is the spring irrigation in May, when each paddy fills with water and becomes a mirror reflecting the sky and hills — a quite different, but equally striking, "mirror terraces" scene.

Best Photo Spot — Fengjiang Terraces Viewing Platform

The Fengjiang Terraces Viewing Platform is the ace vantage point of the whole scenic area — the field layers here are the richest and most dramatic, and it is the single best position for sunrise and sea-of-clouds shots. From the platform you can look down over the entire expanse of golden terraces cascading down the valley.

  • 1. Sunrise & Sea of Clouds — Golden Hour Magic: Arrive before dawn. On clear autumn mornings the cold valley air condenses into a layer of cloud that fills the terraced basin, while the first sunlight lights up the golden rice above the mist — the single most iconic Fengyan shot.
  • 2. Layered Contours — Richest Terrace Layers: No other platform in the park matches this one for the depth of contour layers. The stepped fields fan out below in dozens of curved bands, making it the spot to capture the sheer scale of the 12,000-mu terrace system.
  • 3. Overlooking the Golden Field — Full Panorama: From here you look down over the entire golden terrace expanse — perfect for wide-angle panoramas that take in the whole valley at harvest time.

Detailed Information

Location

Xuanwo Town, Hanyin County, Ankang, Shaanxi Province

Opening Hours

Open all day (free entry)

Best Time to Visit

Mid-to-late September to mid-to-late October for golden rice; May for mirror-water terraces.

Tickets

Free entry

Photography Tips

Arrive before dawn at the Fengjiang platform for sunrise & sea of clouds; carry a wide-angle lens for the full panorama.

Transportation

Bus or drive from Xi'an to Hanyin County (~4 hrs); then local road up to the terraces (~1 hr)
