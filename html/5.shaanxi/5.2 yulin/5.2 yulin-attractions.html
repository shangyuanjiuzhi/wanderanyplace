Yulin Attractions

Discover the most amazing attractions in Yulin, from the Great Wall to desert wonders and ancient frontier heritage

1

Wave Valley (波浪谷)

★★★★☆ 4.8

A stunning geological wonder featuring undulating red sandstone formations carved by millions of years of wind and water erosion. The wave-like patterns create a mesmerizing landscape that rivals America's Antelope Canyon, offering breathtaking photography opportunities at sunrise and sunset.

3-4 hours Jingbian County ¥90/person
Learn More
2

Hongshixia Gorge (红石峡)

★★★★☆ 4.5

Known as the "Forest of Steles of the Great Wall," this scenic canyon features over 160 cliffside stone inscriptions from the Ming and Qing dynasties. The towering red sandstone cliffs, carved with calligraphy and poems by military commanders and scholars, make it a unique cultural and natural treasure.

1-2 hours Yuyang District ¥30/person
Learn More
3

Zhenbeitai Great Wall

★★★★☆ 4.7

The largest and most imposing watchtower on the entire Great Wall of China, standing 30 meters tall with four tiers. Built in 1607 during the Ming Dynasty, it served as the command center for the northern frontier defense, offering panoramic views over the desert and the ancient wall stretching to the horizon.

2-3 hours Yuyang District ¥30/person
Learn More
4

Yulin Ancient City

★★★★☆ 4.5

A well-preserved Ming Dynasty frontier city with ancient city walls, traditional courtyard homes, and the iconic Drum Tower. Wander through historic streets where merchants and soldiers once traded and patrolled, experiencing the unique blend of Han Chinese and nomadic cultures that defined this border town.

2-3 hours Yuyang District Free admission
Learn More
5

Jiang Family Manor (姜氏庄园)

★★★★☆ 4.4

A magnificent Qing Dynasty courtyard complex built by the wealthy Jiang family, often called the "Forbidden City of Northern Shaanxi." This sprawling estate showcases exquisite brick and wood carvings, multi-level courtyards, and the opulent lifestyle of frontier merchants during the Qing era.

1-2 hours Mizhi County Free
Learn More
6

Li Zicheng's Temporary Palace

★★★★☆ 4.3

The imperial-style palace built for Li Zicheng, the rebel leader who overthrew the Ming Dynasty and briefly founded the Shun Dynasty (1644). Perched on a hill in his hometown of Mizhi, the complex features grand halls, imperial architecture, and exhibits about the life and legacy of one of China's most famous peasant revolutionaries.

1-2 hours Mizhi County ¥30/person
