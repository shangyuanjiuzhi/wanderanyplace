Hongshixia Gorge Overview

A comprehensive guide to Hongshixia, a cultural and natural treasure of northern Shaanxi.

Hongshixia (红石峡), also known as the 'Forest of Steles of the Great Wall,' is a scenic canyon located just 3 kilometers north of Yulin city. The towering red sandstone cliffs are carved with over 160 stone inscriptions from the Ming and Qing dynasties, making it one of the largest collections of cliffside calligraphy in China.

The inscriptions were created by military commanders, scholars, and officials who passed through this strategic frontier pass. They feature calligraphy in various styles, including seal, clerical, regular, and cursive scripts. The gorge also features ancient cave temples, a flowing stream, and beautiful natural scenery that blends culture and nature.

Must-see Highlights

  • Saishang Stele Forest (塞上碑林): On the cliffs of both the east and west banks of the gorge, there are more than 120 cliffside stone inscriptions in regular, cursive, clerical, and seal scripts — truly an open-air calligraphy museum. Among them, the inscription "Yuxi Shengdi (Yuxi Scenic Land)" by Zuo Zongtang and "Return My Rivers and Mountains" by the renowned anti-Japanese general Ma Zhanshan are particularly striking.
  • Cave Temples (石窟古刹): Over 40 cave temples are carved into the east cliff, stacked layer upon layer and connected by narrow tunnels such as the "Heaven Gate" and "Earth Gate", creating a hidden world of their own. Some visitors have noted, however, that most of the Buddha statues inside the caves are modern replicas, with the originals largely lost.
