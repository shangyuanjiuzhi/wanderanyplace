Wave Valley Overview

A comprehensive guide to Jingbian Wave Valley, one of China's most stunning geological wonders.

Wave Valley (波浪谷), located in Jingbian County of Yulin, is a breathtaking geological formation featuring undulating red sandstone carved by millions of years of wind and water erosion. The wave-like patterns, formed during the Cretaceous period, create a mesmerizing landscape that rivals America's Antelope Canyon. The rich red and orange hues shift dramatically throughout the day, making it a paradise for photographers.

The valley stretches across several square kilometers, with multiple viewing platforms and hiking trails that allow visitors to explore the intricate rock formations. The most spectacular views can be enjoyed at sunrise and sunset when the low-angle light enhances the texture and depth of the sandstone waves.

Route Recommendations

  • Grand Loop (In-depth Tour, approx. 3–4 hours): Ideal for fit travelers and photography enthusiasts seeking a thorough experience. This route covers nearly all the core attractions, including Flame Danxia, Red Cliff Danxia, the Sky-Slit Gorge, Earth-Core Danxia, and the Kilometer Gallery.
  • Middle Route (Relaxed Version, approx. 2–3 hours): Suitable for most visitors. You'll enjoy the majority of the scenic highlights, such as Flame Danxia and the Sky-Slit Gorge, without completing the full route — a moderate effort.
  • Mini Loop (Quick Tour, approx. 1 hour): Perfect for families with elderly members or children, or travelers short on time. It mainly covers the Flame Danxia area, then returns the same way or via a short boardwalk — very easy going.
