Yulin Local Food

Discover the hearty flavors of northern Shaanxi through 8 iconic Yulin dishes and street snacks

榆林豆腐 (Yulin Tofu)

Description

Yulin's most famous culinary treasure, Yulin Tofu is renowned for being made with sweet local well water — specifically the water from the "Sour Plum Spring." The result is exceptionally tender, white, and fragrant tofu with a delicate texture that melts in the mouth. Unlike ordinary tofu, it carries a subtle natural sweetness and is served simply with soy sauce, chili oil, and scallions to let its pure flavor shine.

Ingredients

1. High-quality soybeans
2. Sweet well water from Yulin
3. Traditional sour milk brine (lactobacillus)
4. Soy sauce and chili oil
5. Scallions and sesame oil

Recommended Restaurants

1. 老榆林豆腐馆 Lao Yulin Tofu Guan

2. 塞上豆腐坊 Saishang Tofu Fang

3. 老街豆腐宴 Laojie Tofu Yan

羊杂碎 (Yangzasui Lamb Offal)

Description

A quintessential northern Shaanxi breakfast, Yangzasui is a hearty lamb offal soup made from lamb intestines, liver, lungs, and blood tofu. Slow-simmered with a rich bone broth and warming spices, it is topped with cilantro, chili oil, and sometimes hand-pulled noodles. The dish is deeply nourishing, fragrant, and perfect for Yulin's cold desert winters.

Ingredients

1. Lamb intestines, liver, and lungs
2. Lamb bone broth
3. Blood tofu and vermicelli
4. Cilantro, chili oil, and garlic
5. Sichuan pepper and star anise

Recommended Restaurants

1. 绥德羊杂碎 Suide Yangzasui

2. 老马家羊杂碎 Lao Ma Jia

3. 塞上羊杂 Saishang Yangza

拼三鲜 (Pinsanxian Assorted Three Fresh)

Description

A festive Yulin banquet dish, Pinsanxian combines pork, lamb, and chicken — the "three fresh" ingredients — braised together with a rich assortment of vegetables and tofu skin. The name "拼" (assemble) reflects the dish's layered, abundant presentation, symbolizing prosperity and reunion. It is often served at weddings and important celebrations in northern Shaanxi.

Ingredients

1. Pork belly, lamb, and chicken
2. Tofu skin and vermicelli
3. Cabbage, carrots, and wood ear mushrooms
4. Ginger, garlic, and star anise
5. Soy sauce and rock sugar

Recommended Restaurants

1. 榆林饭店拼三鲜 Yulin Fandian

2. 陕北拼三鲜 Shanbei Pinsanxian

3. 老街宴 Laojie Yan

炸豆奶 (Zhadounai Fried Soy Milk)

Description

A unique Yulin dessert, Zhadounai transforms ordinary soy milk into a golden, crispy-fried delicacy. Freshly ground soy milk is thickened with starch, chilled into a soft custard, then deep-fried until the exterior is crispy and caramelized while the inside remains creamy and tender. It is often dusted with sugar or drizzled with honey, offering a delightful contrast of textures.

Ingredients

1. Freshly ground soy milk
2. Mung bean or potato starch
3. Eggs
4. White sugar or honey
5. Cooking oil for deep frying

Recommended Restaurants

1. 榆林炸豆奶 Yulin Zhadounai

2. 老街甜品店 Laojie Dessert

3. 塞上小吃 Saishang Xiaochi

炉馍馍 (Lumomo Baked Buns)

Description

Lumomo are traditional baked buns from northern Shaanxi, cooked in a clay oven or "炉" over an open fire. The dough is typically filled with date paste, red bean, or sesame, then baked until the crust is golden and crisp with a soft, sweet interior. These rustic buns carry a smoky aroma from the clay oven and have been a staple snack and festival food in Yulin for generations.

Ingredients

1. Wheat flour and yeast
2. Date paste or red bean filling
3. Sesame seeds and brown sugar
4. Lard or vegetable oil
5. Warm water for dough

Recommended Restaurants

1. 老榆林炉馍馍 Lao Yulin Lumomo

2. 绥德炉馍 Suide Lumomo

3. 老街馍坊 Laojie Mofang

香哪 (Xiangna)

Description

Xiangna is a distinctive Yulin street snack with a name that echoes the local dialect. It is a savory, layered pastry or pancake made from a fermented grain batter, typically cooked on a flat griddle with a generous brushing of oil. The result is a golden, flaky snack with a fragrant, slightly tangy flavor from the fermentation — beloved by locals as a quick, satisfying bite on the go.

Ingredients

1. Mixed grain flour (millet, sorghum, wheat)
2. Fermented batter starter
3. Scallions and salt
4. Chili oil and sesame paste
5. Cooking oil for the griddle

Recommended Restaurants

1. 老街香哪 Laojie Xiangna

2. 塞上小吃铺 Saishang Xiaochipu

3. 老榆林味道 Lao Yulin Weidao

荞面圪坨 (Qiaomian Getuo Buckwheat Noodles)

Description

A signature northern Shaanxi staple, Qiaomian Getuo are small, twisted buckwheat noodles shaped by hand using a special board. The name "圪坨" refers to their knotted, spiral form. Made from pure buckwheat flour, these noodles are gluten-free, nutty, and hearty. They are typically served in a savory lamb broth or tossed with a spicy minced meat sauce, offering a robust, earthy flavor unique to the Loess Plateau.

Ingredients

1. Pure buckwheat flour
2. Warm water for dough
3. Lamb or pork minced sauce
4. Chili oil, garlic, and vinegar
5. Cilantro and scallions

Recommended Restaurants

1. 陕北荞面圪坨 Shanbei Qiaomian Getuo

2. 老榆林面馆 Lao Yulin Mian Guan

3. 定边荞面 Dingbian Qiaomian

粉浆饭 (Fenjiangfan Starch Porridge) Breakfast

Description

Fenjiangfan is a traditional fermented porridge cherished in Yulin and the broader northern Shaanxi region. It is made from the sour liquid left over from making bean starch noodles (粉浆), which is gently fermented and then simmered with millet, rice, and vegetables. The result is a slightly tangy, refreshing, and nourishing porridge — a beloved summer comfort food that aids digestion and cools the body.

Ingredients

1. Fermented bean starch liquid (粉浆)
2. Millet and rice
3. Soybeans and peanuts
4. Pickled vegetables and scallions
5. Sesame oil and chili oil

Recommended Restaurants

1. 老榆林粉浆饭 Lao Yulin Fenjiangfan

2. 塞上粥铺 Saishang Zhoupu

3. 老街早点 Laojie Zaodian
