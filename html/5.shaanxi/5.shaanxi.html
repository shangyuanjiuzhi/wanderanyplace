About Shaanxi Province

Shaanxi Province, located in the heart of China, stretches across the Loess Plateau, Qinling Mountains, and Han River Valley. Its strategic position made it the natural capital region for 14 Chinese dynasties — including the Zhou, Qin, Han, and Tang.

Beyond Xi'an, Shaanxi offers incredible diversity: the dramatic deserts and Great Wall of Yulin, the sacred Mount Hua in Weinan, the panda habitats of Hanzhong, and the revolutionary history of Yan'an. But the journey begins in Xi'an.

Shaanxi Province Itineraries

Plan your perfect Shaanxi journey — from a 3-day Xi'an essence to a 7-day grand tour across the Loess Plateau, Qinling Mountains, and Han River Valley.

3-Day Shaanxi Essence

Xi'an imperial core & sacred Mount Hua

1 Day 1: Xi'an Imperial Capital

  • Terracotta Army — Pit 1, 2, 3 & Bronze Chariots
  • Ming Dynasty City Wall — cycle the 14 km rampart
  • Muslim Quarter — night food street & Great Mosque

2 Day 2: Tang Dynasty Heritage

  • Big Wild Goose Pagoda & Da Ci'en Temple
  • Daming Palace ruins & Tang Museum
  • Shaanxi History Museum (book ahead)

3 Day 3: Mount Hua Day Trip

  • High-speed train Xi'an → Mount Hua (30 min)
  • Cable car up North Peak — ridgeline hike
  • Return to Xi'an for Tang Dynasty Show & dumpling banquet

5-Day Northern Loop

Xi'an, Xianyang & the Yellow River bend

1 Day 1: Xi'an — Terracotta Army

  • Full morning at Terracotta Warriors Museum
  • Huaqing Hot Springs & Lishan backdrop

2 Day 2: Xi'an Old City

  • City Wall cycling & Bell/Drum Towers
  • Muslim Quarter street food crawl

3 Day 3: Xianyang Imperial Tombs

  • Qianling Mausoleum — Wu Zetian's untouched tomb
  • Han Yangling — 3,000 mini terracotta warriors

4 Day 4: Yan'an — Hukou Waterfall

  • Hukou Waterfall — Yellow River narrows to 30 m
  • Overnight in Yichuan County

5 Day 5: Mount Hua

  • Cable car up — cliffside plank walk at South Peak
  • Return to Xi'an, fly out

7-Day Grand Shaanxi Tour

North Loess Plateau to south Qinling & Han River

1 Day 1-2: Xi'an Imperial Core

  • Terracotta Army, City Wall, Muslim Quarter
  • Daming Palace, Big Wild Goose Pagoda

2 Day 3: Xianyang — Qin & Tang Tombs

  • Qianling Mausoleum & Han Yangling
  • Xianyang Museum — Qin bronze collection

3 Day 4: Yan'an — Yellow River & Loess

  • Hukou Waterfall & Yellow Emperor's Mausoleum
  • Ganquan Grand Canyon — "China's Antelope Canyon"

4 Day 5: Mount Hua Sacred Peak

  • Five-Peak ridgeline tour & cliffside plank

5 Day 6: Hanzhong — Qinling Cloud Sea

  • Longtou Mountain cable car & cloud sea
  • Xinghan Heritage Park — Han dynasty culture

6 Day 7: Baoji — Famen Temple Relic

  • Famen Temple — Buddha's finger bone relic
  • Bronze Ware Museum — "China" inscription on He Zun

Travel Tips: Spring (Apr–May) and autumn (Sep–Oct) are ideal — mild weather and clear skies. High-speed rail connects Xi'an to Mount Hua (30 min), Yan'an (2h), and Hanzhong (1.5h). Book Shaanxi History Museum and Terracotta Army tickets 3+ days in advance. Mount Hua plank walk requires gloves and harness rental on-site.
