About Shaanxi Province

Shaanxi Province, located in the heart of China, stretches across the Loess Plateau, Qinling Mountains, and Han River Valley. Its strategic position made it the natural capital region for 14 Chinese dynasties — including the Zhou, Qin, Han, and Tang.

Beyond Xi'an, Shaanxi offers incredible diversity: the dramatic deserts and Great Wall of Yulin, the sacred Mount Hua in Weinan, the panda habitats of Hanzhong, and the revolutionary history of Yan'an. But the journey begins in Xi'an.
