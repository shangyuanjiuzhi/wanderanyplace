Longtou Mountain Overview

A comprehensive guide to Longtou Mountain (龙头山) — a forest park in Nanzheng District, Hanzhong, whose main peak resembles a dragon's head and floats above one of southern Shaanxi's most famous seas of clouds.

Named for its ridgeline that looks like a dragon's head rising above the clouds, Longtou Mountain is the most accessible high-mountain sea-of-clouds viewpoint in southern Shaanxi. The route up is fixed and effortless — a smooth cable car, an outdoor "Cloud Lift" escalator, then a gentle 3.5 km cliff plank road along the spine — with no backtracking the whole way.

May brings slopes of wild azalea; October gilds the mountain with autumn foliage. The cable-car cabins are steady and acrophobia-friendly, making the cloud sea reachable for nearly everyone.

The Classic Three-Step Route — Cable Car + Cloud Lift + Plank Road

Longtou Mountain's play route is fixed and simple — basically a "Cable Car + Cloud Lift + Plank Road" three-step loop, with no backtracking the entire way.

1Visitor Center → Cable Car

After ticket check at the visitor center, ride the cable car straight up the mountain. The cabins are steady and acrophobia-friendly.

2Cable Car → Cloud Lift

Off the cable car, transfer to the outdoor "Cloud Lift" (an escalator) that carries you directly up to the Summit Plaza.

3Summit Plaza → Plank Road

From the plaza, follow the cliff plank road — about 3.5 km, flat and gentle, with multiple viewing platforms to stop, walk, and shoot. The full loop takes roughly 2-3 hours.

Mountain Highlights

Longtou's three signature sights — the sea of clouds from the plank road, the outdoor Cloud Lift escalator, and the steady cable car — together make the high-mountain scenery reachable for nearly every visitor.

  • 1. Sea of Clouds (云海) — Signature View: From the cliff plank road, the ridgeline floats above a rolling cloud sea — the mountain's most iconic sight, best at sunrise.
  • 2. Cloud Lift (云梯) — Outdoor Escalator: A rare open-air escalator carrying visitors from the cable-car station straight up to the Summit Plaza — effortless altitude gain with a view.
  • 3. Cable Car (索道) — Acrophobia-Friendly: Steady, smooth cabins that lift you up the mountain comfortably — friendly even to visitors afraid of heights.
