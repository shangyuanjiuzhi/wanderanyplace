Hanzhong Attractions

From a dragon-head peak above a sea of clouds and "Shaanxi's Jiuzhaigou" gorges to a grand Han-dynasty heritage park — Hanzhong is where the Han empire began and the Qinling meets the Daba ranges

1

Longtou Mountain (龙头山)

★★★★★ 4.6

A forest park whose main peak resembles a dragon's head, rising above a famous sea of clouds. A cable car lifts visitors to the ridgeline, where a glass skywalk and cliff plank road trace the spine of the "dragon." In May the slopes bloom with wild azalea; in October the autumn foliage turns the whole mountain gold. The most accessible high-mountain sea-of-clouds view in southern Shaanxi.

4-5 hours Nanzheng District ¥50 + cable car

2

Liping Scenic Area (汉中黎坪景区)

★★★★★ 4.5

Dubbed "Shaanxi's Jiuzhaigou" — a national forest park blending karst geology, old-growth forest, and waterfalls. The signature "dragon-scale" rock formations ripple across the canyon walls, and the Longshan, Tianjiu, and Huangyang canyons each have their own waterfall and pool. Autumn (late Oct–early Nov) turns the whole valley into a red-and-gold foliage wonderland — the park's most photogenic season.

1 day Nanzheng District ¥75
Learn More
3

Xinghan Heritage Park (兴汉胜境)

★★★★☆ 4.3

A grand cultural tourism park celebrating Hanzhong as the birthplace of the Han dynasty — where Liu Bang was enfeoffed as King of Han before founding the empire. Towering Han-style palaces halls, a lakeside promenade, and immersive galleries retrace the roots of Han Chinese identity. The evening light-and-water show over Hanchen Lake is the highlight; allow half a day, and stay into the night.

3-4 hours Hanzhong City ¥90-¥120
