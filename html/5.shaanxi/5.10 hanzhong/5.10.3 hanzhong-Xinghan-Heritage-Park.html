Xinghan Heritage Park Overview

A comprehensive guide to Xinghan Heritage Park (兴汉胜境) — a Han-dynasty cultural tourism park in Hanzhong city, celebrating Hanzhong as the birthplace of the Han empire where Liu Bang was enfeoffed as King of Han before founding the dynasty.

Sprawling along the lakeshore, the park brings Han culture to life through towering Han-style palaces, immersive galleries, and a Han Culture Museum with a 45 m gilded dome and underwater glazed corridor. The essence of a visit lies in the two signature shows — the indoor epic "Han Song" and the night water spectacle "Legend of Tianhan" — best chosen by your time and interest.

Allow at least half a day, and stay into the night if you can.

Must-See Show 1 — "Han Song" (汉颂) Indoor Epic

The essence of Xinghan Heritage Park lies in its two shows — pick by time and interest. Showtimes may shift by day; Mondays usually dark, so confirm on the official mini-program before going.

  • "Han Song" (汉颂) — Indoor Epic Show: Staged in the Han Culture Grand Hall with a 360° circular stage and mechanical rigs, retelling Han-dynasty stories — Zhang Qian's mission to the Western Regions, Cai Lun's invention of paper — in a visually stunning way. Ideal for visitors who want to truly understand Han culture, and a great pick for families.

Must-See Show 2 — "Legend of Tianhan" (天汉传奇) Night Water Show

A night open-air light spectacle staged on Hanyuan Lake, blending water screens, drones, and fireworks on a grand scale.

  • "Legend of Tianhan" (天汉传奇) — Night Water Show: The storyline is simpler than Han Song, but the atmosphere is unbeatable — and night shots are endlessly photogenic.

Must-Visit Venue 2 — Han Music Bureau (汉乐府) & Exterior Views

A quiet, refined hall to experience Han-dynasty ritual music — hear the bianzhong bells and try hands-on crafts like bamboo-slip rubbing. A serene counterpoint to the big shows. Outside, the Han-style palace and lakeside promenade make for sweeping exterior shots.

  • Han Music Bureau (汉乐府) — Ritual Music & Crafts: Hear bianzhong bells and try bamboo-slip rubbing — a serene counterpoint to the big shows.
  • Exterior Views — Han Palace & Lakeside: The Han-style palace and lakeside promenade make for sweeping exterior shots.

Detailed Information

Location

Hantai District, Hanzhong, Shaanxi Province

Opening Hours

9:00-18:00 (stay into the night for the water show)

Best Time to Visit

Year-round; Monday shows usually dark — check the official mini-program before going.

Tickets

Park entry: ¥90-¥120

Han Song / Legend of Tianhan: separate show tickets

Show Tips

Han Song for deep culture + families; Legend of Tianhan for night atmosphere & photos. Showtimes shift daily; Mondays usually dark.

Transportation

In Hanzhong city — taxi or bus direct to the park gate
