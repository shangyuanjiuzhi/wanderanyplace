Liping Scenic Area Overview

A comprehensive guide to Liping Scenic Area (汉中黎坪景区) — a national forest park in Nanzheng District, Hanzhong, dubbed "Shaanxi's Jiuzhaigou" for its blend of karst geology, old-growth forest, waterfalls, and dragon-scale rock formations.

Spanning canyons carved into the southern Qinling foothills, Liping weaves together karst geology, old-growth forest, and a string of waterfalls. The signature "dragon-scale" rock formations ripple across the canyon walls, and the Longshan, Tianjiu, and Huangyang canyons each hold their own waterfall and pool.

Autumn (late October to early November) turns the whole valley into a red-and-gold foliage wonderland — the park's most photogenic season, when the Maple Forest Waterfall pours through a frame of coloured forest.

Must-See Highlight — Maple Forest Waterfall

Liping's ace attraction — a two-tier waterfall that pours through a frame of coloured forest. In autumn the falls are wreathed in red and gold maple canopy, and the rising mist catches frequent rainbows, making it the park's most photogenic spot.

  • 1. Maple Forest Waterfall (枫林瀑布) — The Ace Spot: Liping's signature sight. A two-tier waterfall cascades from between the coloured-forest canyon, and the mist thrown up often holds a rainbow — endlessly photogenic.
  • 2. Autumn Coloured Canopy — Late Oct to Early Nov: In peak autumn the falls are framed by red and gold maple forest, making the rainbow-and-foliage combo one of Shaanxi's most iconic autumn shots.

Detailed Information

Location

Liping Town, Nanzheng District, Hanzhong, Shaanxi

Opening Hours

8:00-17:30

Best Time to Visit

Late October to early November for autumn foliage; summer for cool forest and full waterfalls.

Tickets

Entry: ¥75

Shuttle bus: ¥40

Photography Tips

Stand at the lower pool for the rainbow-in-mist shot; arrive mid-morning when sunlight hits the spray.

Transportation

Drive from Hanzhong city to Liping (~2 hrs); shuttle bus covers the park's internal loop
