About Hanzhong

The Cradle of the Han Dynasty

Hanzhong sits in the southwestern corner of Shaanxi, where the Qinling range meets the Daba mountains and the Han River winds through a green basin. It was here that Liu Bang was enfeoffed as King of Han before marching out to found the Han dynasty — the very name "Han Chinese" traces back to this city.

Today Hanzhong blends that imperial heritage with dramatic mountain scenery. Longtou Mountain floats its dragon-head ridgeline above a famous sea of clouds, reachable by cable car and escalator. Liping Scenic Area, dubbed "Shaanxi's Jiuzhaigou," packs karst canyons, dragon-scale rocks, and the rainbow-misted Maple Forest Waterfall. And in the city itself, Xinghan Heritage Park recreates the Han empire in palaces, museums, and two signature shows.

Top Attractions

Explore Hanzhong's iconic landmarks — from a dragon-head peak floating above a sea of clouds to "Shaanxi's Jiuzhaigou" gorges, and a grand Han-dynasty heritage park where the empire comes alive

#1 Attraction

Longtou Mountain (龙头山)

A dragon-head peak rising above one of southern Shaanxi's most famous seas of clouds. The fixed three-step loop — cable car, outdoor "Cloud Lift" escalator, and a 3.5 km cliff plank road — reaches the summit with no backtracking. May azalea blooms and October autumn foliage are the highlights.

#2 Attraction

Liping Scenic Area (汉中黎坪景区)

Dubbed "Shaanxi's Jiuzhaigou" — a national forest park blending karst geology, old-growth forest, and waterfalls. The signature "dragon-scale" rocks ripple across canyon walls, and the ace Maple Forest Waterfall throws rainbows in the mist. Late October turns the whole valley red and gold.

#3 Attraction

Xinghan Heritage Park (兴汉胜境)

A grand Han-dynasty cultural park celebrating Hanzhong as the birthplace of the Han empire. Towering Han-style palaces, a Han Culture Museum with a 45 m gilded dome, and two signature shows — the indoor epic "Han Song" and the night water spectacle "Legend of Tianhan." Allow half a day and stay into the night.

Sample Itineraries

Plan your perfect Hanzhong trip with our recommended itineraries

1-Day Hanzhong Highlight

Mountain clouds in the morning, Han heritage at night

1 Morning: Longtou Mountain

  • Cable car up — steady, acrophobia-friendly cabins
  • Cloud Lift escalator to Summit Plaza
  • 3.5 km cliff plank road — sea of clouds viewpoints

2 Afternoon: Xinghan Heritage Park

  • Han Culture Museum — 45 m gilded dome
  • Han Music Bureau — bianzhong bells & bamboo-slip rubbing
  • Lakeside promenade & exterior palace photos

3 Evening

  • "Legend of Tianhan" night water show on Hanyuan Lake

2-Day Hanzhong Deep Dive

Sea of clouds, "Shaanxi's Jiuzhaigou," and the Han empire

1 Day 1: Longtou Mountain & Xinghan

  • Morning — Longtou cable car + Cloud Lift + plank road
  • Afternoon — Xinghan Heritage Park & Han Culture Museum
  • Evening — "Legend of Tianhan" night water show

2 Day 2: Liping Scenic Area

  • Drive to Liping (~2 hrs) — full day in the park
  • Maple Forest Waterfall — rainbow-in-mist shot
  • Longshan, Tianjiu & Huangyang canyons

Note: Longtou Mountain's sea of clouds is best at sunrise; Liping's autumn foliage peaks late Oct–early Nov. Showtimes at Xinghan shift daily; Mondays usually dark — check the official mini-program before going.
