Discover Baoji

Where the word "China" was first cast in bronze, a Buddha's finger relic rests, and the Qinling Mountains reach their highest peak

About Baoji

The Ancient Cradle West of Chang'an

Baoji, on the western Guanzhong plain at the foot of the Qinling range, is the birthplace of the Western Zhou and Qin civilizations. Its soil has yielded China's richest bronze hoards — the He Zun here bears the oldest known inscription of the word "Zhongguo" (China), while the Lai Pan's 372 characters read like a bronze history book of the Zhou kings.

East of the city stands Famen Temple, guardian of a genuine finger-bone relic of the Buddha and 2,499 Tang royal treasures unearthed from its sealed underground palace. To the south rise the forested gorges below Taibai Mountain — the Qinling's 3,767 m main peak — where Honghe Valley offers waterfalls, cliff plank walkways, and a cool summer escape.

Top Attractions

Explore Baoji's iconic landmarks — from a Buddha-relic temple and China's greatest bronze museum to the waterfalls of the Qinling foothills

#1 Attraction

Famen Temple (法门寺)

China's most revered Buddhist reliquary, home to a genuine finger-bone relic of the Buddha and 2,499 Tang royal treasures from its 1,000-year-sealed underground palace. The relic is open for public viewing only in May and October; the old temple is free for worship year-round.

#3 Attraction

Baoji Bronze Ware Museum (宝鸡青铜器博物院)

The largest bronze-ware museum in China, housing 4,870+ Shang and Zhou relics. Its three national treasures — the He Zun (earliest "China" inscription), the 372-character Lai Pan, and the king-of-gui Hu Gui — trace the very origins of Chinese statehood. Free entry.

2 hours Learn More
#4 Attraction

Honghe Valley Forest Park (红河谷森林公园)

A forested gorge at the northern foot of Taibai Mountain: ride the Shenxian Ridge cableway in the morning, then hike Panlong Bay's cliff plank walkways for 1.5 hours to Doumu Waterfall, the "No. 1 Waterfall in Shaanxi." A cool, uncrowded summer escape.

1-Day Baoji Highlight

Buddha relic in the morning, 3,000-year-old bronze in the afternoon

1 Morning: Famen Temple

  • Old Temple & True-Body Pagoda — free worship (1h)
  • Famen Temple Museum — 2,499 Tang treasures (2h)
  • Relic viewing — only in May & October

2 Afternoon: Bronze Museum

  • He Zun — the first written "China" (1h)
  • Lai Pan & Hu Gui — Zhou dynasty bronzes (1h)

3 Evening

  • Return to Xi'an or overnight in Baoji city

2-Day Baoji Deep Dive

Tang Buddhist heritage and Qinling waterfall hiking

1 Day 1: Temple & Bronze

  • Famen Temple — relic, pagoda & underground-palace treasures
  • Baoji Bronze Ware Museum — He Zun, Lai Pan & Hu Gui

2 Day 2: Honghe Valley

  • Morning — Shenxian Ridge cableway & ridge hike
  • Afternoon — Panlong Bay plank walkways (1.5h)
  • Doumu Waterfall — "No. 1 Waterfall in Shaanxi"

Note: Taibai Mountain's main summit area is currently not open to foreign visitors; Honghe Valley at its foot offers a scenic alternative.
