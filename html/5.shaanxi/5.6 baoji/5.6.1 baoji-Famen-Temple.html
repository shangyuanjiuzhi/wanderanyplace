Famen Temple Overview

A comprehensive guide to Famen Temple (法门寺) — China's most sacred Buddhist reliquary, where a genuine finger-bone relic of the Buddha and 2,499 Tang royal treasures emerged from a sealed underground palace in 1987.

Famen Temple in Fufeng County, Baoji, is one of China's most sacred Buddhist sites — revered for enshrining a genuine finger-bone relic (sarira) of Sakyamuni. First built in the late Eastern Han dynasty and expanded through the Tang, it became an imperial pilgrimage destination; Tang emperors escorted the relic to Chang'an seven times, each procession accompanied by lavish offerings sealed inside the temple's underground palace.

That palace lay sealed and forgotten for over a thousand years until its chance rediscovery in 1987, when 2,499 Tang royal treasures emerged in pristine condition — the largest single hoard of Tang imperial artifacts ever found. Today the old temple is free to enter for worship, the on-site museum displays the treasures, and the giant Namaste Dagoba marks the modern complex.

Must-See Highlights

Famen Temple pairs free worship in the old monastery with a world-class museum of Tang royal treasures. Three core viewing points below cover the relic, the museum, and the temple-and-crypt complex — plan your dates carefully, as the relic is not always on display.

  • 1. Buddha's Finger Relic Viewing (佛指舍利瞻礼) — Plan Your Dates: The relic is no longer enshrined in the Namaste Dagoba for daily worship — it has been moved back inside Famen Temple. Under the new rule effective October 2026, the relic is open for public viewing only in May and October each year, on specific dates announced by the temple's official notice. Visit outside these two months and the relic will not be on display.
  • 2. Famen Temple Museum (法门寺博物馆) — Tang Splendor Up Close: Well worth the visit. It concentrates the 2,499 Tang royal treasures unearthed from the underground palace. Star exhibits — the Eight-Fold Reliquary (八重宝函) and the Silver Khakkhara with Twelve Rings (锡杖) — are national-treasure-grade artifacts forbidden from leaving the country, offering a glimpse of the Tang at its peak.
  • 3. Famen Temple & Underground Palace (法门寺与地宫) — Free Worship, Sealed Crypt: The old temple itself is free to enter for worship and to view the Ming-dynasty True-Body Pagoda (真身宝塔). Note, however, that the Tang underground-palace site beneath the pagoda is currently closed; a 1:1 reproduction model can be viewed inside the museum.

Detailed Information

Location

Famen Town, Fufeng County, Baoji, Shaanxi Province

Opening Hours

Old Temple: 8:30-17:30

Museum: 9:00-17:00 (closed Mondays)

Best Time to Visit

May & October for relic viewing; spring and autumn for pleasant weather.

Tickets

Old Temple: Free

Museum: ¥100 (combo tickets available)

Reservation Information

Museum tickets at the gate; arrive early on relic-viewing days as crowds are heavy

Transportation

Tourist bus from Xi'an to Famen Temple (~¥25, 2.5hrs); High-speed rail to Baoji South, then bus to Famen (~1hr)
