Honghe Valley Forest Park Overview

A one-day guide to Honghe Valley Forest Park (红河谷森林公园) — a forested gorge on the northern flank of Taibai Mountain in Mei County, combining a ridge-top cableway hike with a waterfall canyon walk.

Honghe Valley ("Red River Valley") is a forested gorge at the northern foot of Taibai Mountain (太白山), the main peak of the Qinling range. Carved over millennia by the rushing Red River, it packs waterfalls, cliff-hanging plank walkways, and old-growth forest into one compact park — a cooler, quieter alternative to the main Taibai summit.

A classic day pairs two experiences: a morning challenge up Shenxian Ridge (神仙岭) with cableway access, and an afternoon canyon hike through Panlong Bay (蟠龙湾) to Doumu Waterfall (斗姆瀑布) — billed as "the No. 1 Waterfall in Shaanxi."

One-Day Recommended Route

A full-day loop combining a ridge hike and a waterfall canyon. Morning is for the high ground; after the descent, the afternoon is reserved for the shaded valley and its signature cascade.

  • Morning — Challenge Shenxian Ridge (神仙岭): Self-drivers can go straight to the Shenxian Ridge cableway parking lot. Visitors on public transport take the park sightseeing bus (¥15 per person) to the resort area, then walk to the trailhead. Ride the cableway up and hike the ridge for panoramic Qinling views before descending.
  • Afternoon — Canyon Stroll: After coming down, head to the Panlong Bay (蟠龙湾) area. Hike about 1.5 hours along the river and cliff plank walkways to reach Doumu Waterfall (斗姆瀑布) — "the No. 1 Waterfall in Shaanxi (陕西第一瀑)," where the spray-cooled air makes a perfect end to the day.

Detailed Information

Location

Honghe Valley Forest Park, Heyin, Mei County (眉县), Baoji, Shaanxi Province — northern foot of Taibai Mountain

Opening Hours

8:00-18:00 (last entry earlier in winter; check seasonal notices)

Best Time to Visit

May to October — cool summer escape; fullest waterfalls after rain.

Tickets

Entrance: ¥54; Sightseeing bus: ¥15/person; Shenxian Ridge cableway priced separately

Reservation Information

Tickets at the gate or via official channels; cableway subject to weather closure in rain or wind

Transportation

~2h drive from Xi'an; high-speed rail to Baoji South or Mei County, then taxi/bus to the park entrance
