About Weinan

Where the Yellow River Meets the Qinling

Weinan sits in the eastern Guanzhong plain of Shaanxi, where the Wei River flows into the Yellow River and the Qinling range thrusts its granite peaks skyward. This is land steeped in legend — the hometown of historian Sima Qian, calligrapher Yan Zhenqing, and the legendary Yellow Emperor, and the site where the Zhou dynasty's founding rites were born.

Its crown jewel is Mount Hua (Huashan), one of China's Five Great Mountains — a 2,155 m granite massif whose near-vertical trails, knife-edge ridges, and cliff-plank paths have earned it the title "the most perilous mountain under heaven." Beyond Huashan, Weinan offers the old town of Hancheng, the Yellow River's dramatic bend at Tongguan, and Puzhou ancient city.

Know Before You Go — Sky Plank Walk & Sparrow Hawk's Flip

  • Cost: Both require separate tickets — ¥30 per person each.
  • Reservation: Book via the official Huashan Tourism Hotel Management Co. WeChat account. Online orders require 2+ people. Need help? Click here.
  • Solo Travelers: Find a partner at the summit, or join the on-site queue — a few cash-only tickets are held daily.
  • Requirements: Sky Plank: 15+ yrs & 150 cm+. Sparrow Hawk: 160 cm+. Not for those with a fear of heights.
