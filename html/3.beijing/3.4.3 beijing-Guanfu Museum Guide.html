Guanfu Museum Overview

A comprehensive guide to Guanfu Museum, a private museum known for its collection of Chinese antiques.

The Guanfu Museum was founded by Ma Weidu, a famous Chinese collector, and is known for its collection of Chinese antiques, including furniture, porcelain, and works of art from different dynasties.

The museum is located in the Chaoyang District of Beijing and features a diverse collection of artifacts that showcase China's rich cultural heritage. Unlike many public museums, Guanfu Museum is a private institution that offers a more intimate and personalized experience for visitors.

The museum's collection includes rare and valuable items that are not typically found in other museums, making it a unique destination for art and history enthusiasts. It provides insights into the personal collecting journey of Ma Weidu and his passion for preserving Chinese cultural artifacts.

Recommended Tour Route

The Guanfu Museum is best explored by starting from the Furniture Collection exhibition, which features exquisite Chinese furniture from various dynasties.

After exploring the furniture collection, visit the Porcelain and Ceramics exhibition, showcasing rare and beautiful porcelain pieces from different periods of Chinese history.

Don't miss the Guanfu Cats! These adorable cats have official positions and names, such as "Yunduoduo" and "Huangqiangqiang". You might even be lucky enough to catch their "cat party" when they line up for meals in the courtyard!
