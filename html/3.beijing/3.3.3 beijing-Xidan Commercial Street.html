Xidan Commercial Street Overview

A comprehensive guide to Xidan Commercial Street, a bustling shopping district in the heart of Beijing.

Xidan Commercial Street is a major shopping district located in the Xicheng District of Beijing. It has a long history as a commercial center, dating back to the Qing Dynasty, and has evolved into a modern shopping area with a mix of traditional and contemporary stores.

The street offers a wide range of shopping options, from department stores and shopping malls to boutique shops and street vendors. It's a popular destination for both locals and tourists, offering everything from fashion and electronics to traditional Chinese goods and souvenirs.

Xidan is particularly famous for its shopping malls, including Xidan Joy City, Xidan Cultural Square, and Xidan Mall. The area is also known for its vibrant atmosphere, especially in the evening when the streets are lit up with neon signs and the area comes alive with shoppers and diners.

Recommended Tour Route

Xidan Commercial Street is best explored by starting from the northern end near Fuxingmen and walking south towards Xidan Cultural Square. This route allows you to experience the full range of shopping and dining options along the way.

Key attractions include the Xidan Joy City, a large shopping mall with international brands and entertainment options, the Xidan Cultural Square, a public space with cultural events and performances, and the Xidan Mall, a traditional shopping center with a mix of stores and restaurants.

Shopping Tips
