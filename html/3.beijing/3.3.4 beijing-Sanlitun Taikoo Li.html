Sanlitun Taikoo Li Overview

A comprehensive guide to Sanlitun Taikoo Li, a trendy shopping and entertainment district in Beijing.

Sanlitun Taikoo Li is a modern shopping and entertainment district located in the Chaoyang District of Beijing. It is known for its unique architectural design, featuring a mix of modern and traditional elements, and its vibrant atmosphere with a wide range of shops, restaurants, and entertainment options.

The district is divided into two main areas: North Taikoo Li and South Taikoo Li. North Taikoo Li is known for its luxury brands and high-end restaurants, while South Taikoo Li offers a more casual atmosphere with a mix of international and local brands, cafes, and bars.

Sanlitun is particularly popular among young people and expatriates, making it one of the most vibrant and cosmopolitan areas in Beijing. It's a great place to experience the modern side of Beijing while enjoying shopping, dining, and entertainment.

Recommended Tour Route

Sanlitun Taikoo Li is best explored by starting at the South Taikoo Li area and working your way north to the North Taikoo Li area. This route allows you to experience the full range of shopping, dining, and entertainment options available.

Key attractions include the South Taikoo Li, with its casual atmosphere, local boutiques, and cafes, the North Taikoo Li, featuring luxury brands and high-end restaurants, and the Sanlitun Village, a pedestrian area with a mix of shops and entertainment venues.

Don't miss the various art installations and cultural events that are held at Sanlitun throughout the year, as well as the vibrant nightlife scene with bars and clubs that come alive in the evening.

Detailed Information

Location

Sanlitun Taikoo Li,ChaoyangSanlitun Road No.11, 19 (Workers' Stadium Subway Station Entrance & Exit D Pedestrian 240 Meters), Chaoyang District, Beijing, China

Opening Hours
  • Monday to Sunday: 10:00 - 22:00
  • Shops and restaurants may have different opening hours
Shopping Tips
  • Bargaining is not common in Sanlitun Taikoo Li
  • Credit cards are widely accepted
Transportation
  • Metro: Take Line 10 to Sanlitun Station (Exit C) or Line 6 to Dongdaqiao Station (Exit A)
  • Taxi: Directly to Sanlitun Taikoo Li
