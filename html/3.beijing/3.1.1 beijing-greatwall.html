Great Wall Overview

A comprehensive guide to the Great Wall of China, one of the world's most iconic landmarks.

The Great Wall is an ancient military defense project in China, one of the greatest buildings in the world, and a World Cultural Heritage site. Construction of the Great Wall began during the Spring and Autumn Period and Warring States Period. After unifying China, Emperor Qin Shi Huang connected the walls of various states to form the foundation of the Great Wall.

The existing Great Wall was mainly built during the Ming Dynasty, stretching over 21,000 kilometers. It is a symbol of the Chinese nation and a source of pride. The Great Wall is not only a defense project but also a crystallization of the wisdom and courage of the ancient Chinese working people.

The Great Wall is one of the Eight Wonders of the World. From its construction to final completion, it took more than two thousand years, making it a unique spectacle in human history. The Beijing section of the Great Wall is divided into eight sections, with Badaling Great Wall, Juyongguan Great Wall, and Mutianyu Great Wall being the most famous.

Badaling Great Wall

Yanqing District
6:30- 17:00
¥40/person
Apr-Jun, Sep-Oct

Recommended Tour Route

Badaling is the most famous section of the Great Wall both at home and abroad. Divided by the Guan City into "north and south sections," the northern section is the essence of Badaling Great Wall, featuring 12 watchtowers. Among them, North Tower 8 is the highest watchtower in elevation and the best place to overlook the Great Wall. The southern section has 7 watchtowers and features a gentler terrain. The most famous attraction in Badaling is the "Hero Slope" where there's a saying "He who has not climbed the Great Wall is not a true hero." In addition, you can also visit the Museum of the Great Wall of China.

Mutianyu Great Wall

Huairou District
7:30 - 18:30
¥45/person
Apr-May, Sep-Oct

Recommended Tour Route

Mutianyu Great Wall is the longest section of the Great Wall of China, stretching 5,400 meters. Must-see attractions include Zhengguan Terrace and Dajiao Tower. Zhengguan Terrace consists of three hollow watchtowers, each with an observation pavilion on top. Uniquely, the gate is set on the side rather than the front - this peculiar architectural design is rarely seen along the Great Wall. Dajiao Tower features the Great Wall walls on three sides, offering expansive panoramic views of both the interior and exterior of the Great Wall. Climb up to the beacon tower and explore its mysteries.

Taking the cable car provides a panoramic view of the Great Wall. After reaching Watchtower 14, head west all the way to Watchtower 20, the highest point of the Mutianyu Great Wall scenic area. (Estimated climbing time: 1 hour)

Juyongguan Great Wall

Changping District
6:30 - 18:00
¥40/person
Jun - Oct

Recommended Tour Route

Juyongguan Great Wall, located in Changping District about 50km from Beijing, is one of the Great Wall's most important passes. Known for its strategic location and beautiful scenery, it has been a vital military stronghold since ancient times.

Unlike Badaling and Mutianyu, Juyongguan's defining feature is its dangerous terrain - easy to defend and difficult to attack. It has been a contested area and an important defensive line for the ancient capital. Since the Yuan Dynasty, it has been listed as one of the "Eight Scenes of Yanjing" for its breathtaking natural beauty.

The key attractions include the majestic pass city (known as the "First Pass Under Heaven") and the Cloud Platform, a Yuan Dynasty white marble platform with exquisite stone carvings of birds and beasts. Juyongguan serves as the northern gateway to Beijing, embodying the saying "one man guarding the pass, ten thousand men cannot pass".

Summer to early autumn is the ideal time to visit, when the "Juyong Greenery" scenery is at its peak. The entire tour takes about 2 hours, making it possible to visit both Juyongguan and nearby attractions in one day.

S2 Line Flower Sea

This was once part of the Beijing-Zhangjiakou Railway, China's first trunk railway built by Zhan Tianyou. Today, it's a "flower sea train" that runs in spring. In early March, after passing through Juyongguan Tunnel, the train weaves through a sea of flowers. At the foot of the Great Wall, the train and flower sea create a beautiful scenic encounter.

Transportation Information
  • 1、To ride the Juyongguan Flower Sea train: Take Metro Line 8 or 13 to Huoying Station, then walk to Huangtudian Station to take the S2 line (Huangtudian to Yanqing).
  • 2、To photograph the Juyongguan Flower Sea train: Take bus Chang 20 to Juyongguan, then walk to the Juyongguan Flower Sea.
Ticket Prices

Other Great Wall Sections

Huanghuacheng Water Great Wall

Huanghuacheng Water Great Wall is a unique section of the Great Wall that features water views. This section is known for its beautiful combination of the ancient wall and surrounding water bodies, creating a stunning landscape that differs from other Great Wall sections.

The water Great Wall offers a different perspective on this ancient structure, with reflections of the wall in the water creating a picturesque scene. It's less crowded than other sections and provides a more peaceful and contemplative experience for visitors.

Jiankou Great Wall

Jiankou Great Wall is known for its wild and rugged beauty. This section is less developed and more challenging to hike, making it popular among adventure seekers and photographers who want to experience the Great Wall in its original, unrestored state.

The steep terrain and crumbling sections provide a sense of authenticity and adventure. It's recommended for experienced hikers who are prepared for a more demanding trek. The views from the watchtowers are spectacular and well worth the effort.

Simatai Great Wall

Simatai Great Wall is famous for its unique architecture and strategic location. This section features the famous "Simatai Tower," which is one of the most iconic and photographed watchtowers along the entire Great Wall.

The tower is known for its distinctive shape and the stunning views it offers of the surrounding mountains and valleys. Simatai is a great choice for those who want to experience both the architectural and natural beauty of the Great Wall.

Badaling Remnant Great Wall

The Badaling Remnant Great Wall refers to the unrestored and less-visited sections near the popular Badaling area. These sections offer a glimpse into the original state of the Great Wall before restoration efforts.

Unlike the heavily restored main Badaling section, these remnant sections show the wall in its weathered and authentic condition. It's a fascinating contrast to the pristine sections and provides insight into the wall's historical evolution.

Gubeikou Great Wall

Gubeikou Great Wall is known for its historical significance and strategic importance. This section was a crucial defensive point and features well-preserved watchtowers and fortifications.

The area offers a mix of restored and unrestored sections, providing visitors with a comprehensive Great Wall experience. The surrounding countryside and traditional villages add to the cultural richness of a visit to Gubeikou.

Badaling National Forest Park - Badaling Water Pass Great Wall

The Badaling National Forest Park encompasses the Badaling Water Pass Great Wall, offering a unique combination of natural beauty and historical architecture. This area features the wall winding through forested mountains and alongside water features.

The park provides a more peaceful and nature-focused Great Wall experience. Visitors can enjoy hiking trails, forest scenery, and the opportunity to see the wall in a different environmental context than the main tourist sections.
