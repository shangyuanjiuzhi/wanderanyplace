Beijing Attractions

Discover the most amazing attractions in Beijing, from ancient landmarks to modern marvels

1

Great Wall of China

★★★★★ 4.9

One of the Seven Wonders of the World, the Great Wall is a symbol of Chinese civilization. Stretching over 21,000 kilometers, it's an ancient military defense project that showcases the wisdom and courage of the Chinese people.

4-6 hours Yanqing District ¥40/person
Learn More
2

Forbidden City

★★★★★ 4.8

The imperial palace complex of the Ming and Qing dynasties, with over 9,000 rooms and 600 years of history. It's the largest ancient palatial structure in the world and a UNESCO World Heritage site.

3-4 hours Dongcheng District ¥60/person
Learn More
3

Summer Palace

★★★★☆ 4.7

A beautiful imperial garden and summer retreat with stunning lake views and traditional pavilions. Covering 290 hectares, it's renowned for its harmonious blend of natural scenery and traditional Chinese architecture.

2-3 hours Haidian District ¥30/person
Learn More
4

Temple of Heaven

★★★★☆ 4.6

A masterpiece of Chinese architecture and a UNESCO World Heritage site. Built in the 15th century, it was where emperors prayed for good harvests and is famous for its Hall of Prayer for Good Harvests.

2-3 hours Dongcheng District ¥34/person
Learn More

Tiananmen Square

★★★★☆ 4.5
