Beijing Local Specialties

Discover the delicious local specialties of Beijing, from traditional dishes to modern interpretations

老北京涮羊肉 Beijing Hot Pot

Description

Beijing Hot Pot is one of the most representative traditional cuisines in Beijing, featuring copper pot-boiled lamb. Premium Inner Mongolian lamb is sliced paper-thin and boiled in boiling clear broth, then dipped in special sesame paste seasoning for an incredibly delicious taste.

How to Eat

1. Place the sliced lamb pieces in the boiling copper pot and cook for 10-15 seconds
2. Remove and dip in sesame paste, chive flowers, fermented bean curd and other seasonings
3. Eat with sweet garlic to reduce greasiness and enhance flavor
4. After finishing the meat, you can cook vegetables and noodles in the broth

Recommended Restaurants

东来顺 Donglaishun Restaurant

Address: No. 198 Wangfujing Street, Dongcheng District

南门涮肉 Nanmen Hot Pot

Address: No. 12 Nancaiyuan Street, Xicheng District

北京烤鸭 Peking Duck

Description

Peking Duck is one of Beijing's most famous delicacies with a long history. Force-fed ducks are selected and undergo special roasting techniques to achieve crispy skin and tender meat with overflowing aroma. It is typically served with lotus leaf pancakes, scallion strips, cucumber strips, and sweet bean sauce.

How to Eat

1. Place a lotus leaf pancake on your hand
2. Add several pieces of duck skin and duck meat
3. Add scallion strips and cucumber strips
4. Spread sweet bean sauce
5. Roll up the pancake and eat

Recommended Restaurants

Address: No. 30 Qianmen Street, Dongcheng District

大董烤鸭 Dadong Roast Duck

Address: No. 22 Dongsi Shitiao, Dongcheng District

炸酱面 Zhajiang Noodles

Description

Zhajiang Noodles is a traditional Beijing noodle dish featuring fried bean paste sauce made from yellow bean paste and pork belly cubes. The noodles are chewy and served with cucumber strips, bean sprouts, green beans and other seasonal vegetables for a rich and delicious taste.

How to Eat

1. Cook the noodles and drain the water
2. Pour the fried bean paste sauce over the noodles
3. Add various vegetable strips
4. Mix well and eat

Recommended Restaurants

Address: No. 138 Wangfujing Street, Dongcheng District

炙子烤肉 Zhizi Roast Meat

Description

Zhizi Roast Meat is a traditional Beijing delicacy that uses a special zhizi (iron plate) as a cooking tool. Sliced lamb and scallion strips are placed on the hot plate for roasting, then dipped in special seasonings. The aroma is overwhelming and the taste is delicious.

How to Eat

1. Place the sliced lamb and scallion strips on the zhizi for roasting
2. Flip the meat when it changes color and continue roasting
3. Dip in soy sauce, vinegar, sesame seeds and other seasonings
4. Wrap in shaobing or lotus leaf pancake and eat

Recommended Restaurants

烤肉季 Kaorouji

Address: No. 14 Qianhai East Bank, Xicheng District

烤肉宛 Kaorouwan

Address: No. 5 Niujie Street, Xicheng District
