Longqingxia Overview

A comprehensive guide to Longqingxia, a beautiful gorge area with waterfalls and outdoor activities.

Longqingxia is a beautiful gorge area located in Yanqing District, Beijing. Known for its stunning waterfalls, clear rivers, and outdoor activities, it's a popular destination for nature lovers and adventure seekers.

The area features a series of gorges, waterfalls, and rock formations, offering visitors a chance to escape the city and enjoy nature. It's particularly beautiful during spring when flowers bloom and autumn when leaves change color.

The scenic area includes famous Longqing Gorge, Waterfall Valley, and various other natural attractions. It's a perfect place for rafting, hiking, and enjoying the outdoors.

Recommended Tour Route

Longqingxia is best explored by following a route that covers main attractions and outdoor activities. Start at the main entrance and proceed through the scenic area.

Key attractions include the Longqing Gorge, Waterfall Valley, and various outdoor activities such as rafting and hiking.

The area is particularly beautiful during spring and autumn when the weather is pleasant for outdoor activities. Don't miss the opportunity to experience rafting on the river and explore the unique gorge formations.
