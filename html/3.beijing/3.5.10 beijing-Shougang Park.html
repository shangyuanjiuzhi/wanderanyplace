Shougang Park Overview

A comprehensive guide to Shougang Park, a former steel mill transformed into a modern urban park with industrial heritage and Olympic elements.

Shougang Park, located in the Shijingshan District of Beijing, was once the site of the Shougang Group, one of China's largest steel mills. After the 2008 Beijing Olympics, the steel mill was relocated to Hebei Province, and the site was transformed into a modern urban park.

The park preserves many industrial heritage buildings and structures, including blast furnaces, steelmaking equipment, and workshops. It also features the Big Air Shougang, a freestyle skiing and snowboarding venue built for the 2022 Winter Olympics.

Today, Shougang Park is a popular destination for both locals and tourists, offering a unique blend of industrial history, modern culture, and Olympic legacy.

Recommended Visit

Shougang Park is best explored by starting at the main entrance and following a path through the park's key areas. The park's layout is designed to guide visitors through a sequence of spaces, each offering a different experience of industrial heritage and modern culture.

Key areas to visit include the Big Air Shougang, a freestyle skiing and snowboarding venue built for the 2022 Winter Olympics, and the industrial heritage zone, which includes preserved blast furnaces, steelmaking equipment, and workshops. The park also features cultural venues, restaurants, and shops.

Don't miss the opportunity to explore the park's lakeside areas, which offer scenic views of the industrial structures and the surrounding mountains. The park's various trails and paths make it a great place for walking, cycling, and photography.
